## Decisions made (Glen, this session)

- Initiative is the **mandatory root**; everything nests under it. Data uniformly 5-level; visibility per client.
- Type change **cascades** the whole subtree by the same level offset.
- Contracting depth **hides levels in UI, preserves in DB** (reversible).
- Depth config is **per-client** (NBI stays 4-level visible; Couch Heroes activates Initiative).
- Initiatives keep the **same field set** as other types (research on Jira/Linear/Shortcut supported this; evergreen initiatives just leave dates empty).
- Hidden levels render as a **clean skip** -- no indicators.
- Pill dropdown offers **all active levels**; protection is an **undo toast (~10s)**, not a modal.
