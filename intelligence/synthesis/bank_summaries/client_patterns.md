## What This Bank Knows

- **Common failure modes in 40-100 person founder-led studios** -- documentation gap hidden by enthusiasm, single producer failure, chain-of-command bypass, scope invisibility, garden leave eligibility gaps, contractor compliance gaps, founder shadow conversations creating misaligned leads (item 12 in What Clients Hide), and nine further patterns of "what clients hide"

- **Founder communication and shadow conversation patterns** -- Managing founder midnight ideas (shared idea log, weekly review, never ad hoc); shadow conversation silo pattern (founder briefs each lead separately, creating parallel unconnected threads; dedicated ideation channel with mandatory lead-posts routing rule; game director asks the founder to route through the channel, not the other way around; sensitive HR matters remain bilateral)

- **Estimation failure patterns** -- estimate inflation as default behaviour (sequential challenge; "shenanigans" call-out; tier language); scope capitulation (constraint escalation fix; flashpoint log); VS staffing efficiency ratings (DoD must precede estimates; lead estimation rated 1-5)

- **Pre-production and VS patterns** -- live service vs box game mindset gap (including "commitment without comprehension" CEO variant: investor-sold-not-operationalised; internal live service director without champion; investor review as real decision inflection); VS real game anxiety; poisoned phrase problem; junior vs senior mindset diagnostic (response to incomplete builds is the seniority tell)

- **Meeting governance** -- four-layer cadence (exec / studio leadership / product council / leads); decision owner model; executive RAG format; executive meeting accountability redesign (Excel tracker replaces AI summaries; silence as enforcement mechanism; locked weekly tabs; C-suite only as standing members)

- **Executive coaching and feedback methodology** -- sledgehammer/screwdriver/scalpel tiered feedback model: private sledgehammer sets the anchor; screwdriver adjusts calibration; scalpel is target-visible-only in mixed company; public correction without private anchor is ineffective and damages credibility; passive/subtle feedback consistently fails -- directness is required more often than coaches expect; escalation ladder (give information → observe capacity → raise directness); founder coaching goal is channelling native leadership style, not replacing it

- **Contractor and employment compliance** -- IR35: £60K per prohibited leave payment; label evidence risk; gross-up day rate methodology (216 billable-days formula for income-neutral reform; monthly soft cap with pre-approval gate; mid-year settlement approach); Belgium/Spain/Germany also exposed. Right-to-work checks for UK contractors routinely missed. Immigration/sponsorship documentation must precede application. Fintech banking not accepted in regulated UK payroll contexts. Graceful vs swift exit protocol; multi-jurisdiction IP assignment gap for long-tenure contractors

- **People, talent, and leadership** -- 80/20 mid-senior/junior target; dual-path TA recruiter model (embedded FTC + external contingency for 10+ role windows; 9-month combined cost beats 12-month single-TA hire; hiring plan as external recruiter unlock; disagree-and-commit framing for stakeholder disagreement); hire slowly fire fast; ATS workflow; staged staff replacement; strike-based re-engagement protocol

- **Headcount and hiring advisory** -- scope-first headcount decision sequence (three-step gate: scope cut → deprioritise → headcount; Jira math required; MoSCoW lock before estimates; velocity unreliable for first 3 months); QA Lead above strong IC; tech artist hiring misfire (engine-depth vs Maya-tools; panel composition failure; ~147K movable-not-static objects as consequence); CTO vs Technical Director distinction (hiring sequencing: VS needs TD, funding round needs CTO)

- **Publisher and external relationship patterns** -- major Chinese publisher alpha gate (production plan absence is primary failure risk; too-big-to-fail dynamic; Western acquirer fallback also requires clean production plan); publisher analytics control via embedded data scientists (access capture play; studio countermove via remote-only proposal; independent analytics layer as non-negotiable)

- **Analytics tooling and delivery patterns** -- default adoption vs deliberate evaluation (Superset over Power BI; unprompted deployment as validation signal); delivery scope mismatch (UXR session granularity vs cohort-day architecture; flag-without-blocking transfers risk; AER pivot framework); analytics leadership transition mandatory written handover

- **Client fundraising patterns** -- Greek investor relationship-first timeline (budget double; insider-delegated closing; SAFE simplicity for small tranches; pre-closing readiness at press-the-button status)

- **Engagement delivery and advisory methodology** -- milestone as advisory lever; written decision records; red-teaming all NBI outputs; SoW with measurable acceptance criteria; scope fear containment; S-curve change management; three-day offsite; blind affinity estimation; pillar vs value-creation razors framework; dual-mode operating contract for creative directors; audience-first game design; status deck review; red-pink-list onboarding framework

- **Executive feedback toolbox: sledgehammer/screwdriver/scalpel** (source: not_7pWBMRvnbfBop8, internal) -- tiered feedback methodology for coaching studio executives and managing founder behaviour in mixed-company settings; private sledgehammer conversation must establish the anchor before any public technique is deployed; screwdriver adjusts calibration; scalpel is in mixed company but visible only to the target; passive/subtle feedback consistently fails -- coaches under-estimate how often explicit directness is required; escalation ladder (information → observe capacity → raise directness); sticky note coaching supplement (three keywords left of monitor; mitigate tunnel-vision over-indexing by prompting the other word); founder coaching goal is to channel native style, not replace it.

- **Studio ideation silos: shadow conversations and ideation channel fix** (source: not_3bO0Su9glXKHUa, anonymised) -- founder-CEO sharing ideas bilaterally with each lead creates shadow conversations; leads make positions based on different information and contradict each other in group settings; founder believes alignment was reached when only individual conversations happened; fix is a dedicated ideation channel with a mandatory routing rule: leads post received ideas (not the founder, to preserve the relationship); game director asks the founder directly to route through the channel; sensitive HR matters remain bilateral; integrated as a sub-pattern of "Managing Founder Midnight Ideas" with a new What Clients Hide item (item 12).
