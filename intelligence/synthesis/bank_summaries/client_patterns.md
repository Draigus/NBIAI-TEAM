## What This Bank Knows

- **Common failure modes in 40-100 person founder-led studios** -- documentation gap hidden by enthusiasm, single producer failure, chain-of-command bypass, scope invisibility, garden leave eligibility gaps, contractor compliance gaps, founder shadow conversations creating misaligned leads; publisher-funded studio clients do not surface account survival risk until a publisher executive visit is imminent (item 13 in What Clients Hide); 13 total "What Clients Hide" items

- **Publisher and external relationship patterns** -- major Chinese publisher alpha gate (production plan absence is primary failure risk; too-big-to-fail dynamic; fallback acquirer also requires clean production plan); publisher executive hands-on visit as binary account survival review (build stability is the only metric; advisory window is limited once scheduled; downstream staffing trigger may follow immediately); publisher analytics control via embedded data scientists (access capture play; studio countermove via remote-only proposal; independent analytics layer as non-negotiable)

- **Estimation failure patterns** -- estimate inflation as default behaviour (sequential challenge; "shenanigans" call-out; tier language); scope capitulation (constraint escalation fix; flashpoint log); VS staffing efficiency ratings (DoD must precede estimates; lead estimation rated 1-5)

- **Founder communication, shadow conversation, and executive coaching patterns** -- Managing founder midnight ideas (shared idea log, weekly review, never ad hoc); shadow conversation silo pattern (founder briefs each lead separately; dedicated ideation channel with mandatory lead-posts routing rule); sledgehammer/screwdriver/scalpel tiered feedback model (private anchor principle; passive feedback consistently fails); manager 1:1 frequency as group dynamic safety mechanism (1:1 cadence is the calibration mechanism that makes informal dynamics safe in group settings; when it drops, group comments land as performance signals)

- **Contractor and employment compliance** -- IR35: £60K per prohibited leave payment; gross-up day rate methodology (216 billable-days formula; monthly soft cap with pre-approval gate; mid-year settlement); right-to-work gaps routinely missed; multi-jurisdiction IP assignment gap for long-tenure contractors; graceful vs swift exit protocol

- **People, talent, and leadership** -- 80/20 mid-senior/junior target; dual-path TA recruiter model (embedded FTC + external contingency for 10+ role windows; hiring plan as external recruiter unlock; disagree-and-commit framing); interview AI-assistance detection and practical task protocol (uniformly generic correct answers across all topics is the key signal; practical task backstop if doubt remains); QA Lead above strong IC; tech artist hiring misfire (engine-depth vs Maya-tools; panel composition failure); hire slowly fire fast; ATS workflow; staged staff replacement; strike-based re-engagement protocol; studio promotion band structure as prerequisite to individual promotions (band structure must precede any individual promotion; multi-department sign-off; limited slots per cycle prevent grade inflation)

- **Analytics tooling and delivery patterns** -- default adoption vs deliberate evaluation (Superset over Power BI; unprompted deployment as validation signal); delivery scope mismatch (UXR session granularity vs cohort-day architecture; flag-without-blocking transfers risk; AER pivot framework); analytics leadership transition mandatory written handover

- **Engagement delivery and advisory methodology** -- NBI subcontractor margin model (~33% on specialist engagements; solo onboarding then joint client session; "can close" filter); milestone as advisory lever; written decision records; red-teaming all NBI outputs; SoW with measurable acceptance criteria; scope fear containment; S-curve change management; three-day offsite; blind affinity estimation; pillar vs value-creation razors framework; dual-mode operating contract for creative directors; audience-first game design; status deck review; red-pink-list onboarding framework

- **Meeting governance** -- four-layer cadence (exec / studio leadership / product council / leads); decision owner model; executive RAG format; executive meeting accountability redesign (Excel tracker; silence as enforcement; locked weekly tabs)

- **Client fundraising patterns** -- Greek investor relationship-first timeline (budget double; insider-delegated closing; SAFE simplicity for small tranches; pre-closing readiness)
