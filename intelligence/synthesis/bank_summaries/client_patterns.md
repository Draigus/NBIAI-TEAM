## What This Bank Knows

- **Common failure modes in 40-100 person founder-led studios** -- documentation gap hidden by enthusiasm, single producer failure, chain-of-command bypass, scope invisibility, garden leave eligibility gaps, contractor compliance gaps (not visible until a claim surfaces), and eight patterns of "what clients hide" that NBI diagnoses on entry

- **Estimation failure patterns** -- estimate inflation as default behaviour (sequential challenge compresses estimates; art defaults to shipped quality; "shenanigans" call-out as structural fix; tier language resolves at definition level); scope capitulation (constraint escalation fix; flashpoint log as monitoring layer); VS staffing efficiency ratings (efficiency per person, lead estimation capability rated 1-5, DoD must precede estimates)

- **Pre-production and VS patterns** -- live service vs box game mindset gap, VS real game anxiety (scope anxiety and scope creep are the same phenomenon), poisoned phrase problem, VS staffing scope framing ("how much can we close this month?" not a fixed date)

- **Meeting governance** -- four-layer studio meeting cadence (exec / studio leadership / product council / leads; legal/HR at exec layer required; project-level status framing removes defensive posturing); decision owner model for meeting bloat (named owner per stage, attendees restricted to decision makers); executive RAG meeting format (action-item-first, triage only)

- **Contractor and employment compliance** -- IR35: £60K per prohibited leave payment; "vacation"/"paid leave" labels in any system (ClickUp, HiBob, Jira, spreadsheet) constitute tribunal evidence; gross-up day rate calculation (~226 working days/year, ÷ ~18 working days/month); contractors "notify" not "request"; recruiter verbal promises create binding obligations; Belgium/Spain (€55K)/Germany also exposed; prior settlement signals more exposure in same contractor population

- **People and talent** -- 80/20 mid-senior/junior target (20,000+ Steam releases means "good enough" fails); director assessed on two dimensions (technical output + leadership/feedback-giving); technically strong but leadership-weak directors are as risky as technically weak; phased departure in groups of 2-3 with replacement pipeline open before notification; hire slowly fire fast; ATS workflow (5-candidate threshold, scorecard automation); staged staff replacement methodology

- **Engagement delivery and advisory methodology** -- milestone as advisory lever for funded studios, written decision records as mandatory deliverable, red-teaming all NBI outputs, SoW with measurable acceptance criteria, scope fear containment, S-curve change management, three-day offsite pattern, blind affinity estimation, CTO assessment for live service studios

## Most Recent Additions (2026-06-26, 5 new extracts)

- **Contractor compliance: IR35 and day rate model** -- a ~65-person studio had £120K exposure from two simultaneous violation types for a single contractor; day rate gross-up calculation removes vacation billing risk; language change from "paid leave" to "out of office" is non-negotiable; pilot with 3 trusted contractors before rollout; recruiter must have scripted response to "do you offer vacation?"
- **IR35 misclassification evidence types** -- "paid leave" label in any tracking system is tribunal evidence; approval workflows where contractors "request" leave imply employer control; recruiters making verbal promises in interviews create binding obligations; correct response: "your rate is structured to cover time you won't be billing; we do not offer vacation"
- **Four-layer studio meeting cadence** -- exec / studio leadership / product council / leads; mixed audience suppresses hard conversations; project-level status framing ("Investment round = red" not "Legal = red") removes defensive posturing and focuses on solving the constraint; legal/HR attendance at exec layer required initially, optional as trust builds
- **80/20 staff mix and director assessment** -- 80% mid/senior target; director rating across two dimensions: command presence/direct negative feedback + technical output; technically strong but leadership-weak directors create false confidence; rapid improvement path: anonymous staff feedback in structured 1:1, clear behavioural target, defined review date
- **VS staffing model and lead estimation calibration** -- efficiency ratings per person (0-100%) assigned by leads before rolling into a staffing model; lead estimation capability rated 1-5 with evidence before trusting numbers; estimates made without a DoD default to full launch quality; "how much of the VS can we close this month?" is the correct leadership framing
