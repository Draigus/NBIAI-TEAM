# Contract Clerical Error: Addendum Letter Over Full Amendment

## Key Content

Decision framework applied at a ~60-person MMO studio for a minor clerical contract error:

**The situation:** An employment contract contained an incorrect level designation (one grade below the employee's actual seniority). No salary difference was involved. The error was discovered post-signature. The employee wanted documented confirmation of their correct seniority for professional record purposes.

**Option 1 -- Full amendment:** Void the original contract, have the director of the employing entity (a family-held Cypriot company) sign off, reissue. Process involves a specialist law firm. Overhead assessed as disproportionate to the error.

**Option 2 -- Addendum letter (chosen):** CPO drafts a short letter confirming the correct seniority level. Addendum sits alongside the original contract. No legal process, no law firm, no entity director involvement. The original contract stands.

**Decision basis:** The addendum letter satisfies the employee's documented need (proof of seniority) without triggering the full legal overhead of voiding and reissuing a signed contract. Where no salary or financial terms are in error, the addendum is the proportionate response.

**Secondary issue flagged:** The communicating manager's tone (passive-aggressive DMs with the employee) was adding unnecessary friction and morale impact on a trivial administrative issue. The lead's concern was that responsiveness signals respect, even when the fix is small.

## Decisions / Insights

- Studio CPO decided: addendum letter (not contract void and reissue) is the correct remedy for a clerical error with no financial impact
- Studio CPO concluded: where the error is documentary only (level title, not compensation), the addendum satisfies the employee's legitimate need without legal overhead
- Studio lead observed: manager communication tone on HR admin matters affects morale independently of the outcome; a prompt, warm resolution matters even when the issue is trivial

## Context

Working session between a CPO and a senior art lead at a ~60-person UK/Cyprus MMO studio, July 2026. Clerical error discovered post-signature on a contract processed through a Cypriot entity. The entity's director sign-off requirement made full amendment disproportionately complex.

## Applicability

Relevant when: a studio has a clerical error in a signed employment contract (wrong title, wrong level, typo) with no financial impact -- addendum letter is faster and proportionate; full void-and-reissue is reserved for errors with legal or financial consequence.
Relevant when: advising on contract remediation at a studio using a foreign entity (Cypriot, Irish, etc.) -- director sign-off requirements and specialist law firm involvement make full amendment costly; addendum is the default for minor errors.
Relevant when: a studio manager is handling minor HR admin with passive-aggressive communication -- the lead's morale concern is legitimate; even small fixes should be communicated with care, especially when the employee had a reasonable expectation.
