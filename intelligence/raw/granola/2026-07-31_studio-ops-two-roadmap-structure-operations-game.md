# Studio Ops: Two Separate Roadmaps -- Operations and Game -- Not One Combined View

## Key Content

A ~60-person studio in pre-production adopted a two-roadmap structure after finding that a single roadmap mixed task-level game work with ops projects into noise.

**Operations Roadmap:** HR, Finance, IT, Legal projects. Owner per initiative, sub-tasks, due dates, RAG status. Maintained by ops leads (HR lead, finance lead, legal). Milestones: headcount plan, investment prep, certificate of sponsorship, IT infrastructure, tool migration.

**Game Roadmap:** pillars, scope, feature prioritisation, vertical slice delivery. Maintained by production and game direction. Milestones: VS1, VS2, VS3, playtesting gates.

**Hierarchy:** initiative layer sits above project layer. Projects roll up to initiatives. Example: HR initiative contains recruiting, onboarding, and contracts projects. This is the level that appears in a board summary or investor update.

**Priority rule at department level:** production roadmap is priority 1 across all departments -- it blocks everything downstream (hiring prioritisation, finance runway calc, ops roadmap, milestone dates).

**Investment prep** (data room, pitch deck, elevator pitch): C-level priority 1, not delegated below that level.

**Tool integration:** initiatives from both roadmaps imported into project management tool (e.g. WorkSage, Jira) with cascading task structure; ops leads confirm owner, sub-tasks, and due dates per initiative before bulk import.

## Decisions / Insights

- CPO decided: two top-level roadmaps (Operations, Game) replace the single ad hoc setup -- ops and game teams cannot effectively track each other's work
- CPO decided: initiative layer is required above project layer for board-level visibility
- CPO decided: production roadmap is priority 1 across all departments -- it is the blocking dependency for ops, finance, and hiring

## Context

Work prioritisation session between CPO and program management at a ~60-person UK/Cyprus MMO studio, July 2026. Studio moving from ad hoc project tracking to structured dual roadmap ahead of Series A fundraising and September VS1 target.

## Applicability

Relevant when: advising a growing studio (40-100 people) on internal roadmap structure -- a single roadmap becomes noise; separate ops and game views at initiative level are needed.
Relevant when: building ops infrastructure for a studio approaching a funding round -- investor updates require initiative-level ops visibility, not task-level detail.
Relevant when: setting up a project management tool for a studio -- initiative layer above projects is necessary for cross-functional reporting; task level alone does not support board communication.
Relevant when: priority-setting across departments -- production roadmap as the universal priority 1 is a defensible framing when everything downstream depends on milestone dates.
