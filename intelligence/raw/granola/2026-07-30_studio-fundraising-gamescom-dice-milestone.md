# Studio Fundraising Milestone Sequence: Soft Pitch → Full Pitch → Legal Close

## Key Content

Three-phase fundraising roadmap with hard event dates adopted at a ~60-person UK/Cyprus MMO studio in pre-production:

**Phase 1 -- Gamescom (21 August 2026): Soft pitch.** Assets required: one-pager, pitch deck, high-level revenue ranges (not full projections). Purpose: warm investor relationships, test the narrative. Revenue ranges are a scoping exercise separate from the full financial model.

**Phase 2 -- DICE Europe (September 2026): Full pitch readiness.** Assets required: pitch deck finalised, data room 100% complete, fundraising formally begins. This is the moment the round opens. All materials must be consistent with Gamescom assets (same investor audience overlaps).

**Phase 3 -- Post-DICE: Legal framework deadline TBD.** Investors will not commit on day one; the legal framework for closing the round is prepared after the full pitch cycle. Timeline to be agreed once investor conversations begin.

**Data room ownership structure:** CPO-built skeleton in shared workspace, with leads owning their sections: finance/projections lead (financials), ops/HR lead (headcount), legal lead (legal docs), CEO/CPO (overall).

**Financial model scope:** Revenue ranges only for Gamescom (not full projections). Full model for DICE. Revenue stream assumptions require a separate sync before Gamescom.

**Consistency rule:** Materials used at Gamescom and DICE must be consistent -- the same investor audience is likely to attend both.

## Decisions / Insights

- Studio CPO decided: Gamescom is the soft-pitch deadline; DICE is the full-pitch readiness deadline; legal close timeline follows investor conversations
- Studio CPO decided: revenue ranges (not full projections) are sufficient for Gamescom; headcount plan takes priority over the financial model as a first deliverable
- Studio CPO decided: data room built in collaborative workspace with per-section ownership distributed to functional leads

## Context

Operations planning meeting at a ~60-person UK/Cyprus-based MMO studio, July 2026. Attendees: CPO, ops/HR lead, finance lead, legal/ops lead, external program management support. Studio is ~6-10 weeks from VS1 completion, preparing for Series A fundraising process beginning at Gamescom.
