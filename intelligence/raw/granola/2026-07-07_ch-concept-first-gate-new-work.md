# Concept-First Gate for New Art Work

## Key Content

A studio where environment and character artists had been bypassing concept sign-off (going directly to AI generation or 3D builds) established a mandatory concept-first gate for all new work.

**Policy established:**
- No work proceeds in character or environment art without at least one concept pass first
- Concept artists reframed as guardians of art style adherence, not illustrators providing reference only
- Teams bypassing concept by going straight to AI or direct builds is the identified root cause of style drift

**Concept team situation:**
- Team was running at 25-35% capacity and feeling sidelined
- Root cause: direct builds and AI shortcuts had cut them out of the production flow upstream
- Fix: restore concept to the start of the pipeline, not as a retrospective check

**Team sizing (simultaneous decision):**
- Studio reduced concept team from 5 to 2 (keeping highest output, most versatile, AI-friendly artists)
- One additional artist reclassified to creative department (marketing assets, pitch decks) rather than production pipeline
- Right-sizing concept team to match its actual gating role; smaller team with clearer mandate

**Note -- complement to prior extract:**
- This gate applies to NEW work going forward
- Previously approved assets already in production are not sent back for retrospective concept sign-off (see extract from 2026-07-06 on concept as support function)
- Together: concept gates new work upstream; does not block approved assets already in build

## Decisions / Insights

- Leadership decided: mandatory concept pass required before any new character or environment art work begins; no exceptions.
- Leadership decided: concept artists are art style guardians, not illustrators; their approval role is upstream, not decorative.
- Leadership decided: concept team right-sized from 5 to 2 to match a cleaner, enforced gate role.
- Leadership identified: AI-generation and direct-to-3D shortcuts as the structural cause of concept team underutilisation and style drift.

## Context

Leadership alignment session at a ~55-person MMO studio, 7 Jul 2026. Art pipeline friction had been accumulating over several weeks; concept team morale was low. Decision taken alongside team size reduction to clarify the concept function's mandate. Complements the 2026-07-06 extract on concept art as support function (which addressed existing approved assets).

## Applicability

- Relevant when: a studio's art style is drifting and concept artists are underutilised -- the root cause is likely upstream bypass, not concept team quality.
- Relevant when: advising on concept team sizing -- the right size is determined by the gating role, not by the number of assets in production; a smaller team with a clear mandate outperforms a large team without one.
- Relevant when: AI art generation is in use at a studio -- establish whether it bypasses concept approval upstream; if so, style drift is the likely outcome.
- Relevant when: a studio has both a concept gate problem and a pipeline approval problem -- treat them separately: the gate policy addresses new work; retrospective approval of existing work is a separate (and usually wrong) intervention.
