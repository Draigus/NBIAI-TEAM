# Studio Finance Function Setup: Clean Start Over Historical Cleanup

## Key Content

When a growing studio's financial history is inaccessible or unreliable, the correct decision is to start fresh with a clean structure rather than consuming a new finance lead's time on historical reconstruction.

**Decision framework:**
- If historical data is held by someone who is slow to release it and the foundations are murky, a clean-start model is faster and less risky than building on corrupted records
- Hire a short-term contractor to untangle legacy data if needed; do not let the new finance function's first months be consumed by cleanup
- Set two parallel tracks: (1) meet all near-term compliance deadlines regardless; (2) build a clean forward-looking P&L and cash flow model from scratch

**P&L model structure that works for a ~60-70 person studio:**
- Payroll vs. non-payroll costs, broken down by department and cost nature
- Summary views: company-wide and per department (headcount growth, spend by function)
- Headcount plan as the input: status by role, colour-coded by priority (pull-forward vs. push-back) within a capped budget
- Producers booked 100% against their respective department cost lines (not to a general overhead pool)

**RAG operations cadence for finance:**
- Weekly operations meeting with RAG status board on screen; each owner states what they need or why something is blocked
- Framing: Red = needs action, not failure. Train the team on the distinction before the cadence begins.
- Finance lead provides runway and P&L status as a standing agenda item

**Software and subscriptions:** often a hidden cost area in studios; assign a producer or admin resource to chase all heads of department for software lists and seat counts before building the budget model.

## Decisions / Insights

- Studio CPO decided: stop waiting for historical data cleanup; build the forward-looking model from a clean structure immediately.
- Studio CPO observed: a room of people involved in creating the financial chaos probably cannot untangle it; if foundations are wrong, the whole structure will collapse.
- Head of Finance and CPO agreed: platform work deprioritised entirely for the budget model -- game delivery is survival; platform without a game is not.
- Studio CPO decided: short-term contractor for historical data untangling is preferable to consuming Head of Finance's first months on cleanup.

## Context

1:1 between Head of Finance and CPO at a ~70-person MMO studio, 15 Jul 2026. Studio was in a transitional period: new Head of Finance recently joined, historical financial data held by a prior stakeholder and slow to be released. Immediate compliance deadlines (tax and statutory filings) existed regardless of the historical cleanup state. The P&L and runway model was being built from scratch.

## Applicability

Relevant when: a studio brings in a new finance function and historical data is inaccessible or unreliable -- assess within the first two weeks whether cleanup is feasible or whether a clean-start model plus contractor for legacy untangling is faster.
Relevant when: a studio's weekly operations run on gut feel rather than financial transparency -- a RAG status board meeting where Red = needs action (not failure) creates the right incentive to surface problems early.
Relevant when: building a headcount plan for a growing studio -- structure by department with explicit pull-forward/push-back flags within a budget cap; link all producers to department cost lines, not overhead.
Relevant when: auditing a studio's software spend -- seat-count-by-department is almost always unknown; assign admin to chase this before it becomes a surprise cost in any budget model.
