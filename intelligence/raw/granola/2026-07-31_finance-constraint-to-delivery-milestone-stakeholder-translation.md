# Finance-to-Milestone Translation: Stakeholders Need Delivery Consequences, Not Burn Rate Data

## Key Content

Middle management and production leads cannot act on burn rate figures. Sharing raw financial data with them creates noise and anxiety without enabling better decisions.

**The translation:** extract the consequence of the financial constraint and communicate only that.

- **Wrong:** "Our burn rate is £X/month, runway is Y months."
- **Right:** "Because of burn rate, I need this build by [date] for the investment round."

The CPO/CFO's job is to perform this translation. The production team needs a milestone, a date, and the reason why that date is non-negotiable. They do not need the financial inputs.

**Applied example:** finance/ops lead wants milestone dates and production alignment, not raw runway figures. The frame: "we need the vertical slice by September because that is when investor conversations begin" -- this is actionable. "We have 8 months of runway" is not.

**Pattern applies to any financial constraint:** cash pressure, investment gate, revenue milestone -- translate into the delivery commitment it requires, then communicate the commitment.

## Decisions / Insights

- CPO concluded: burn rate stays with finance/leadership only; what middle management receives is the delivery consequence of that constraint
- CPO stated: "Because of burn rate, I need this build by X for investment" is the correct framing for production communication
- Program management noted: finance lead wants milestone dates and production alignment, not raw financial data -- confirmed by CPO

## Context

Catch-up between CPO and program management at a ~60-person UK/Cyprus MMO studio, July 2026. Context: investor prep underway, headcount plan blocking multiple workstreams, production team needs clear milestone targets without financial context they cannot act on.
