# Executive Meeting Accountability Redesign: Excel Tracker over AI Summaries

## Key Content

A redesign of a studio executive meeting format, replacing AI-generated summaries with a structured shared spreadsheet and a built-in accountability mechanism.

**Problem with AI summaries:** not being read by most participants; only one attendee engaged with the shared file. Verbosity without structure.

**New format (shared Excel tracker):**
- Red/yellow/green status per person per week
- Mitigations for reds included inline
- Asks listed explicitly (what each person needs from others)
- Hirings/people updates shown as a screen-shared list in the meeting, not a 20-minute walkthrough
- Outcomes logged at the bottom of each week's tab
- Previous tabs locked once the week closes
- Contents fed into the company AI brain for historical decision tracking

**Accountability mechanism:**
- If any attendee arrives without their section filled, the meeting pauses in silence until they complete it
- No verbal prompting, no excusing -- silence is the enforcement mechanism
- This removes the need for repeated requests and makes the expectation self-reinforcing

**Meeting composition:**
- C-suite only as standing attendees (CEO, COO, CPO, CTO, Head of HR)
- Other leads called in by topic, not as standing members
- Separate studio leadership meeting from executive meeting (agreed in principle)
- Note-taker: senior advisor owns for first few weeks, then hands off

**Transition:** senior advisor owns note-taking initially to model the format before handoff.

## Decisions / Insights

- Glen decided: replace AI summary format with shared Excel tracker; silence is the enforcement mechanism for missing sections, not verbal reminders.
- Glen observed: the AI summary format failed because it was not structured -- participants could not quickly identify what was red, what needed their attention, or what had been decided.
- Glen concluded: previous tabs locked after each week is the mechanism that creates a permanent, searchable decision record without requiring a separate minutes process.

## Context

1:1 between senior advisor and COO at a ~55-person MMO studio, Jul 2 2026. The studio was running weekly executive meetings with AI-generated summaries that were not being read. One director had engaged with the shared file; all others had not. The redesign was agreed in principle to take effect immediately.

## Applicability

- Relevant when: a studio's executive meeting format is producing summaries that no one reads -- the diagnostic is whether participants can answer "what was red last week?" without consulting notes.
- Relevant when: verbal prompting is the only enforcement mechanism for pre-meeting prep -- silence as the structural enforcer removes the social awkwardness of chasing and makes the expectation self-reinforcing.
- Relevant when: a long hiring or people walkthrough is consuming disproportionate executive meeting time -- screen-sharing a pre-prepared list replaces the narrative summary.
- Relevant when: a studio needs a historical decision record -- locking previous tabs and feeding into a knowledge base converts the meeting tracker into a searchable audit trail.
