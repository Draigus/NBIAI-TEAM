# Roadmap as Dual-Purpose Artifact: Investor AMA and CEO Scope Lock

## Key Content

A game studio roadmap serves two distinct but compatible purposes, and the format must serve both simultaneously:

**Purpose 1 -- Investor AMA readiness:**
- Shows rough direction of travel: what epics are in scope, approximate sequencing, when key milestones close (vertical slice, alpha, launch window)
- Answers the investor question: which year does this ship in, and why?
- Not a precise schedule -- a "ballpark roadmap, iterating as we go" is explicitly correct and more credible than false precision

**Purpose 2 -- CEO scope lock:**
- Every feature addition by the CEO must drag a bar out visibly on the chart
- The visual cost of scope creep becomes immediate and undeniable: "you just moved launch back by six weeks" is visible on the chart, not a verbal argument
- Replaces the recurring negotiation over whether a feature is "just a small addition"

**Build method for a studio without detailed scheduling data:**
- Establish a standard process per feature type (e.g. standard 3C work takes X weeks of engineer time at Y headcount)
- Apply a lookup table to estimate input requirements per feature
- Use resource planning data already gathered (headcount by team, existing feature priority spreadsheet) as inputs
- EP or Production lead builds the structure; design and engineering sign off on estimates

**Sequencing outputs:**
- Must/should/could prioritisation pass across all epics feeds directly into the roadmap
- Non-negotiables (features that are off the table for the current milestone) must be declared explicitly before prioritisation begins -- otherwise every item enters as negotiable

## Decisions / Insights

- Studio CPO decided: roadmap serves two masters -- AMA-ready artifact AND scope lock for the CEO; these purposes are compatible and the format must serve both.
- Studio CPO decided: the "every addition drags a bar out" mechanic is the scope lock; the visual cost of scope creep must be immediate and undeniable, not negotiable.
- Studio EP decided: roadmap built using standard-process-per-feature-type lookup rather than bottom-up estimation; faster and sufficient for a ballpark roadmap.

## Context

Weekly strategic session between studio CPO and Executive Producer at a ~70-person MMO studio, 15 Jul 2026. Studio was approaching a vertical slice milestone with investor meetings planned for late September. A roadmap artifact was needed for AMA readiness. The CPO had observed recurring scope additions from the CEO (each framed as small) that were collectively displacing the milestone; the scope-lock mechanic was designed to make the cumulative impact visible.
