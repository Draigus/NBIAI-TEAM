# Two-Template Contractor Policy: Permanent vs. Temporary Bespoke Arrangements

## Key Content

Contractor contract policy codified at a ~60-person UK/Cyprus MMO studio, July 2026:

**Two templates going forward:**
- **Permanent contractors:** 216-day average clause (UK IR35 compliance mechanism). Standard terms, consistent across all long-running arrangements.
- **Temporary/bespoke contractors:** Lighter template (no 216-day clause, no monthly cap, termination at will with ~1 week notice, deliverables framed broadly by output per period). Reviewed case by case each time. For short-term, project-specific, or specialist outsource arrangements.

**Why the split:** Treating every contractor engagement with permanent contractor terms adds compliance overhead to short-term or project-specific arrangements that don't warrant it. Bespoke template allows speed and flexibility for temporary augmentation.

**Deliverable framing for temp contracts:** Broad framing (e.g., "deliver [output type] monthly at [rate]/day") avoids micro-specifying work scope, which creates renegotiation surface. Addendum used for extensions beyond initial term.

**Rollout mechanics:** HR system (HiBob) takes ~2 weeks to configure for the new policy. Policy goes live for new hires in October. Existing contractors mapped separately by HR lead.

**Manager training requirement:** Day-tracking, approval process, and how to explain the policy to candidates. A scripted "offer speech" was requested to ensure consistent candidate communication -- past issues arose from candidates not understanding vacation-in-salary structures.

## Decisions / Insights

- Studio CPO decided: two-template contractor system (permanent 216-day vs. bespoke temp) replaces a single catch-all approach
- Studio CPO concluded: scripted offer speech is a prerequisite for hiring manager rollout -- inconsistent verbal explanation of contractor terms is a recurring source of onboarding friction
- Studio ops lead assigned: map rollout separately for existing vs. new contractors; configure HR system before first pay cycle of implementation month

## Context

Operations meeting at a ~60-person UK/Cyprus-based MMO studio, July 2026. Policy formalised after several temp/bespoke contractor onboardings were processed ad hoc. Five US-based environment art contractors onboarded same day using the bespoke template, providing an immediate test case.
