# NBI Embedded Program Manager in Client Team: Scope and Operating Model

## Key Content

NBI program management support deployed within a client studio's operations team, July 2026:

**Role scope:** Artifact building, roadmaps, project plans, tracking owners and dates, chasing deliverables. The PM does not make decisions -- they structure the client's own decisions into trackable, visible artifacts. The CPO transfers knowledge and context into Workspaces; the PM maintains and updates it.

**Operating cadence:** Weekly check-ins with the ops team, quarterly reviews, roadmap visible at the start of every ops meeting. The PM joins ops meetings in-session and updates artifacts live.

**Tool:** Client's own collaborative workspace tool (Workspaces/Atlassian equivalent), not NBI tooling. The PM operates inside the client's environment, not a separate advisory layer.

**NBI PM's relationship to client leads:** Each functional lead (HR, finance, legal) owns their section of the plan. NBI PM supports the lead in structuring it, not in owning it. The HR lead is the named owner of the contractor policy project; the NBI PM supports her in structuring that project, not in running it.

**Context transfer mechanism:** CPO walks the PM through the full program plan explicitly (roadmap, owners, timelines) so the PM can maintain continuity without needing CPO involvement in every update cycle.

## Decisions / Insights

- NBI CPO decided: embed PM to build structure and maintain artifacts, not to take decisions or own deliverables -- ownership stays with client leads
- NBI CPO decided: PM should be introduced to the full client ops team and given live access to the workspace tool, not siloed in a separate reporting channel
- NBI CPO concluded: the value of an embedded PM is context transfer velocity -- getting CPO knowledge out of Glen's head and into a structured artifact that the client can run without him

## Context

Operations meeting at a ~60-person UK/Cyprus-based MMO studio, July 2026. NBI embedded a junior PM (Magnus Pryer, NBI) into the client's ops function to support roadmap management and deliverable tracking across the Gamescom/DICE fundraising timeline. Client team had previously been running without structured program management.
