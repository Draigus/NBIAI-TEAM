# AI-Assisted Concept Art: Two Senior Controllers Replace Five-Person Team

## Key Content

Headcount strategy adopted at a ~60-person MMO studio in vertical slice phase:

**The shift:** Original concept art headcount plan required 5 concept artists. AI tooling enabled a strategy of AI-assisted base generation with two senior concept artists acting as "controllers" -- creative directors of the AI output rather than hands-on volume producers. Revised headcount: 2 (with 10% probability of needing a third).

**What "controller" means in practice:** Senior concept artists prompt and direct AI-generated base imagery, then apply art direction, consistency enforcement, and pillar-specific refinement. The skill requirement shifts from volume execution to creative judgment and prompt-level art direction.

**Why it works at this studio stage:** Vertical slice concept art requirements prioritise design clarity and investor impression over final-art fidelity. AI-assisted base art is sufficient for VS-level asset generation; the bar rises for early production.

**Risk:** Environment art headcount numbers were flagged as not updated since a key departure (an art lead who left mid-plan). Headcount assumptions in adjacent art departments may need revalidation.

## Decisions / Insights

- Studio CPO decided: concept art team reduced from 5 to 2 by adopting AI-controller model; risk 10% of needing a third
- Studio CPO concluded: the value of a senior concept artist in an AI-assisted pipeline is creative judgment and art direction, not volume output -- the role description and hiring criteria must reflect this shift
- Studio CPO flagged: environment art headcount data was stale due to an unplanned departure; adjacent art plans should be revalidated after any significant headcount change

## Context

Operations meeting at a ~60-person UK/Cyprus-based MMO studio, July 2026. Vertical slice phase, scope now locked. Concept art was identified as a risk area where AI tooling provided a headcount reduction opportunity. Decision made in context of broader headcount prioritisation and tight timelines to Gamescom.
