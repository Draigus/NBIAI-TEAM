# Director Headcount Evaluation: Testing Director-Level Logic

## Key Content

A CPO at a ~55-person studio runs headcount review meetings as a leadership assessment tool, not just a resource planning exercise.

**The headcount meeting format:**
- Each director presents their own headcount logic with hours-based justification
- Facilitator (CPO) MCs but does not provide answers
- "Honey-pot" approach: ask for headcount to test whether directors know where the game actually is; directors who cannot answer reveal themselves
- Independent observer (senior EP or external) attends but does NOT participate or intervene; observes only

**The post-meeting evaluation:**
- Facilitator and observer compare notes privately after the meeting
- Key question: "Did they use director-level logic to arrive at their number?" -- NOT whether they picked the right number
- Outcome per director: coaching plan or replacement plan based on whether they demonstrated the right reasoning process

**Studio Leadership meeting format (companion structure):**
- Named deliberately not "production meeting" to avoid siloing as production-only
- EP chairs and executive-produces: keeps people on track, runs it formally
- RAG status per department: only red and yellow items get substantive airtime
- Green items: self-contained 30-second updates ("I have X, I'm doing Y about it, any objections?") -- brief and complete
- Avoids extended updates that only a fraction of the room cares about
- Requires a working session between CPO and EP before launch to define format; do not roll out cold

## Decisions / Insights

- CPO decided: headcount meetings are leadership assessment tools; the test is director-level logic, not the specific number chosen.
- CPO decided: independent observer attends headcount meeting silently; facilitator does not provide answers or leading questions.
- CPO decided: post-meeting debrief between CPO and observer determines per-director next step -- coaching or replacement.
- Studio leadership decided: new leadership meeting named "Studio Leadership" (not production meeting); EP chairs; RAG status format; green items get 30-second self-contained updates only.
- Studio leadership decided: CPO and EP need a working session to define format before rolling out to the studio.

## Context

Planning session between CPO and EP at a ~55-person MMO studio, 8 Jul 2026. Headcount meeting scheduled for the following day. The Studio Leadership meeting target launch was the week of 20 Jul 2026, subject to EP availability.

## Applicability

- Relevant when: advising a studio that suspects its directors are not operating at the right level -- headcount meeting with observer debrief is a diagnostic that surfaces whether director-level logic is present.
- Relevant when: a studio's production meeting is bloated with mixed audiences and extended updates -- RAG-only format with green as 30-second self-contained items is the structural fix.
- Relevant when: setting up a first structured leadership meeting at a studio -- define format in a CPO/EP working session before launch; do not roll it out cold.
- Relevant when: a CPO needs evidence to make a per-director coaching vs replacement decision -- the headcount logic test provides observable evidence rather than relying on instinct.
- Relevant when: a headcount meeting risks being driven by a facilitator who fills gaps for directors -- honey-pot approach (ask the question, provide no answers) surfaces who can and cannot operate independently.
