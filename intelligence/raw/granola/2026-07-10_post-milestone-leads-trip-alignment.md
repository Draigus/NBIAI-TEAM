# Post-Milestone Leads Trip as Studio Alignment Tool

## Key Content

Target moment: immediately after vertical slice delivery. Trip serves as transition ritual -- "we just finished vertical slice, now we've actually started."

Agenda structure (planned, not template):
1. AI-assisted studio layout session: what does the physical setup look like when the studio moves location
2. Financial reality session: trajectory required to land the next investment round -- intentionally partially ambiguous to preserve healthy pressure
3. Growth rhythm setting: leads co-create what the next phase looks like, rather than receiving it top-down

Format: interactive workshop, not lecture. Leads need to feed into it.

Budget estimate for a 6-person leads trip from distributed UK/EU locations: ~€10k. Considered worthwhile as a milestone investment.

Reference point used by Glen to calibrate ambition: Mike Morhaime (Blizzard founder) paid payroll on personal credit card twice before landing French investor backing. Used to reframe financial difficulty as a stage, not a crisis.

## Decisions / Insights

- Glen concluded: financial ambiguity at the leads level serves a purpose -- full transparency would remove productive pressure; strategic partial disclosure is the right calibration.
- Glen concluded: the post-vertical-slice window is the right moment to reframe the company's trajectory because the team has concrete evidence of capability and the milestone creates psychological permission to talk about the future.
- Glen decided: Greece preferred over UK for the trip for timing and atmosphere (late September, post-VS).

## Context

Couch Heroes operational planning, July 2026. Discussion of logistics and agenda for a leads offsite planned for late September 2026 following vertical slice delivery. Names and specific role identifiers removed. Budget detail included as it is planning-useful and non-identifying.

## Applicability

Relevant when: a studio completes a major milestone (vertical slice, demo, beta) and needs a shared reset moment before the next phase.
Relevant when: a consultant or CPO needs to set a growth rhythm with senior leads without lecturing -- offsite format gives space for co-creation.
Relevant when: leads are distributed across multiple countries -- model the agenda around the "arrival" feeling rather than a standard meeting.
Relevant when: financial disclosure to senior team is a judgment call -- the partial-ambiguity approach is a documented working alternative to full transparency.
