# Confluence/Wiki Governance: Leads as Space Owners, Producers as Coaches

## Key Content

Wiki governance model deployed at a ~65-person studio during Confluence rollout:

**Space ownership:** Department leads own their Confluence spaces. All new pages require lead approval before going live. Producers have no page ownership; they are workflow coaches, not content gatekeepers.

**Personal space first:** New content is created in a personal Confluence space. Once reviewed and approved by the relevant lead, it is promoted to the main department space. This creates a quality gate before content becomes canonical and prevents noise accumulating in the main space.

**Producer role:** Producers train and coach leads on workflow discipline; they do not manage individual pages. The underlying assumption is that leads lack the organisational discipline to enforce consistent standards without coaching -- producers build that discipline, they do not substitute for it.

**Agile training:** Structured agile training planned across leads to build systematic thinking about workflow; this is the foundation that makes wiki governance sustainable at scale.

**Known risk:** Leads with low organisational discipline may not consistently enforce space ownership. Resolution: ongoing producer coaching cadence, not a one-time onboarding briefing.

## Decisions / Insights

- Studio CPO decided: leads own Confluence spaces and approve pages; producers coach workflow discipline but do not manage pages
- Studio CPO identified: leads will fail to enforce governance consistently after a one-time briefing -- producer coaching must be an ongoing cadence, not a setup task
- Studio CPO applied: personal space to main space promotion pathway prevents the canonical space becoming a dumping ground; content must earn its place through a lead approval gate

## Context

Leadership sync at a ~65-person MMO studio, July 2026. Confluence rollout targeting the following week; Jira was already configured with teams categorised and vertical slice tasks being imported. Governance model designed to prevent the wiki becoming unusable while not overloading production with page management.

## Applicability

Relevant when: a studio is rolling out Confluence and deciding who owns what -- the lead-as-space-owner model with producer coaching puts accountability where the knowledge lives, not in production overhead.
Relevant when: a studio's existing wiki is chaotic or inconsistently maintained -- diagnose whether ownership is diffuse (no leads accountable) or whether lead discipline is too low to enforce it; these have different fixes.
Relevant when: advising on Jira/Confluence rollout sequencing -- Jira first (teams, boards, task import) then Confluence (skeleton, personal space policy, lead approval workflow) prevents a governance vacuum during tool transition.
Relevant when: producers are absorbing wiki maintenance alongside delivery work -- the "producers as coaches, not page owners" model redistributes ongoing maintenance burden to leads where the domain knowledge actually lives.
