# Art Outsourcing RFP Framework: Availability as the Primary Filter

## Key Content

When a studio needs external art resourcing at speed (vertical slice pressure), the evaluation framework shifts from capability-first to availability-first.

**Primary filter: Can they start immediately?**
Studios unable to start immediately are cut from consideration before capability, cost, or style fit is assessed. Urgency overrides quality of relationship.

**Studio types evaluated:**
- Boutique studios with style alignment (preferred for stylistic fit but smaller capacity)
- Large global studios with offices across regions (capacity, but less style specificity)
- Hybrid model studios (rent artists from a database with option to hire full-time) -- preferred middle ground when individual contractors aren't available at speed

**Individual contractors preferred over full studios** when candidates can be sourced quickly. Studios are the fallback when contractors cannot be assembled fast enough.

**RFP scope decomposition:** request separate estimates per discipline -- animation, environment art, asset building. Combining them into a single brief obscures whether the studio can cover all needs or just part of them.

**Internal meeting setup:** art director + executive producer + external producer (not game director, not lead designer). The art director drives the brief, the EP controls budget, the producer tracks delivery cadence.

**Decision call cadence:** RFP out mid-week, responses compared, decision call same week. Do not extend the cycle for studios that miss the turnaround window.

## Decisions / Insights

- Studio leadership decided: any studio that cannot start immediately is eliminated before capability assessment
- Studio leadership decided: hybrid rent-to-hire model is the preferred outsourcing structure when pure contractors are unavailable
- Studio leadership decided: RFPs must be split by discipline (animation, environment, assets) to get comparable and actionable quotes
- Studio leadership observed: individual contractors are preferred over studios but hybrid model is an acceptable and closer middle ground

## Context

1:1 between embedded CPO and art director at a ~55-70 person MMO game studio. 2026-07-24. Studio approaching vertical slice with art resourcing gaps identified across animation, environment art, and asset building. Multiple studios approached for bids in parallel. Decision expected same week.

## Applicability

Relevant when: a studio needs external art resources on a tight vertical slice timeline -- availability is the gating filter, not style or cost.
Relevant when: structuring an art outsourcing RFP -- separate estimates per discipline give actionable data; combined estimates obscure gaps.
Relevant when: choosing between individual contractors and studios -- run both tracks in parallel; contractors preferred, hybrid studio is the fallback.
Relevant when: advising on RFP meeting structure -- art director + EP + producer is the right room; game director and lead designer create scope creep.
