# AI Adoption Reducing Concept Art Demand: Art Bible Enables AI Substitution

## Key Content

At a ~55-person MMO studio with a maturing art bible, AI adoption by senior leads has materially reduced traditional concept art demand. The team is operating at approximately 35% concept art utilisation.

**Mechanism:** once an art bible is established, AI tools can follow it with sufficient fidelity to produce usable concept-quality output. This reduces the volume of work routed to human concept artists, particularly for environment and supporting asset work.

**Practical team sizing implication:**
- One concept artist per major domain (environment, character) is now considered sufficient at studios with a strong art bible
- Five concept artists on a ~55-person team is assessed as too many; the excess creates inconsistency as each artist brings a different style
- More concept artists does not improve style consistency -- it degrades it if not tightly directed

**Related observations:**
- AI-generated content requests now arriving from outside the art team (investor pitch decks, marketing materials)
- Senior leads adopting AI directly is a qualitative change: previously AI replaced junior tasks; now it is displacing mid-level concept work
- Pure concept artistry as a standalone skill is assessed as less uniquely valuable once a strong art bible exists

## Decisions / Insights

- Studio CPO observed: AI can follow an established art bible, reducing the unique value of pure concept artists.
- Studio CPO observed: a team of five concept artists at this studio size is excessive; one per domain is the revised assessment.
- Studio leadership observed: concept art utilisation dropping to ~35% is a leading indicator of AI displacement, not a workload trough.
- Studio leadership observed: having more concept artists than needed creates style inconsistency, not consistency.

## Context

Senior 1:1 between CPO and lead art director at a ~55-person MMO studio, July 2026. AI tooling adoption already embedded in the studio's workflow; art bible in place. Concept art team still at original headcount from pre-AI planning. Discussion prompted by low utilisation and redundant parallel work.

## Applicability

- Relevant when: a studio's concept art team is under-utilised -- diagnose AI adoption by leads as the likely cause before assuming it's a workload trough.
- Relevant when: advising a studio on concept team sizing -- one senior concept artist per major domain is the current benchmark at studios with a mature art bible.
- Relevant when: a studio does not yet have an art bible -- building one is now an AI-leverage precondition, not just a style-consistency tool.
- Relevant when: a studio is debating whether to hire a concept artist -- ask first whether AI tools are already covering that output with existing staff.
- Relevant when: a studio reports style inconsistency across concept output -- more concept artists is not the fix; tighter direction and fewer artists with a strong art bible is.
