# Live Service Alignment Failure: Prestige-Quality Studio Leadership vs. Retention-First Design Requirements

## Key Content

Pattern observed at a live service racing game studio (Tencent-funded, ~9-12 months from release): studio leadership is committed to prestige quality metrics (85-90+ Metacritic, 30M units) but not mentally aligned to the 7-year live service revenue model the investor sold them on. The creative director is fully consumed by alpha quality gate with no bandwidth for live service design direction. The live service director has built a detailed live service concept internally but has no champion at leadership level.

Core failure mode: investor (not developer) sold the CEO on live service 3-4 years earlier when no other funding was available. CEO committed without understanding what live service actually requires. Studio then organised around the CEO's quality instincts (prestige, single-player craft mentality) rather than retention-first systems design.

Advisory pattern: the internal live service leader knows the gap and is working around leadership rather than through it. This is unsustainable and will collapse when the investor demands a revenue plan.

## Decisions / Insights

- NBI MD observed: when a studio's CEO is focused on box score metrics and Metacritic, live service is effectively unsponsored regardless of the funding mandate.
- NBI MD observed: the creative director serving as quality gatekeeper at alpha (not as live service champion) is a leading indicator that live service design is not embedded in production culture.
- NBI MD concluded: the game's live service viability depends on the investor's review meeting (August), not on internal design alignment -- the decision point is external, not internal.
- Pattern: "commitment without comprehension" -- leaders who say yes to live service under funding pressure but never operationalise what it requires create the gap between mandate and execution.

## Context

Advisory check-in with a Lighthouse Studios director on 17 Jul 2026. Client is an analytics-embedded engagement; the live service alignment failure emerged as the real strategic risk during the conversation. The studio has an August investor review meeting as a hard inflection point. NBI's role in this context is analytics advisory, not production advisory.

## Applicability

Relevant when: assessing a studio's live service readiness -- ask who is championing live service design at leadership level, not whether a live service concept exists.
Relevant when: advising a founder/CEO who committed to live service under investor pressure -- the first diagnostic is whether they understand session-based retention design vs. unit sales mentality.
Relevant when: a live service director reports no traction with leadership -- this is a structural problem (CEO incentive misalignment), not a communication problem.
Relevant when: pitching NBI live service advisory -- this pattern is common at studios transitioning from premium to GaaS under investor pressure; NBI can diagnose and frame the CEO alignment problem.
