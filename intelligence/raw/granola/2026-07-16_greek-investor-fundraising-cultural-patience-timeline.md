# Greek Investor Fundraising: Cultural Timing and SAFE Simplification

## Key Content

Greek investors in a gaming startup round move on a different timeline to UK or US investors -- not due to process inefficiency, but because relationship and trust-building is itself a substantive step in the process. Budget double the time for prep, discussion, and closing.

**Practical implications:**
- Warm relationship management through an insider (someone with an existing personal relationship to the investor or family office) is the most effective route. Delegate the closing work to the person with the relationship, not to the founder directly.
- Pre-closing preparation: have all resolutions, DocuSign items, and legal paperwork prepared to "press the button" level before any team member takes leave or the founder's attention moves elsewhere. Closing stalls if the mechanism is not ready when the investor is ready.

**SAFE structure lesson:** a small tranche investment (e.g. £50K) requested via a complex SAFE structure with multiple review rounds is disproportionate administration. A qualified legal counsel can identify and call out unnecessary complexity, get the investor to agree to proceed, and sort the paperwork post-transaction rather than pre-transaction. Simple beats complicated for small tranches; the investor's willingness to proceed is more valuable than process perfection.

**Family office dynamics:** family offices often move through a single trusted contact rather than a committee process. Identify and service that contact; do not try to navigate to the committee directly.

## Decisions / Insights

- General counsel observed: SAFE structure complexity for a small tranche was unnecessary; a direct push to proceed and sort paperwork post-transaction resolved a multi-week stall in days.
- Founder/CPO decided: delegate family office closing work to the team member with the existing relationship; direct founder involvement is not required once the relationship pathway is established.
- General counsel concluded: Greek investor cultural timeline requires double the expected duration; this is a planning input, not a problem to solve.

## Applicability

Relevant when: advising a studio on a fundraising round with Greek or Southern European family office investors -- build a timeline with double the standard close duration; assign relationship management to whoever has the warmest existing connection.
Relevant when: reviewing a SAFE structure for a small investment tranche -- a complex SAFE with multiple review cycles costs more in legal time and relationship friction than the complexity protects; counsel should flag and simplify before distributing documents.
Relevant when: a studio is mid-raise and a key legal or financial team member is going on leave -- require all closing-ready documentation to be at "press the button" status before departure; partial prep is worse than no prep.
Relevant when: a studio's investor relationship runs through a family office -- identify the single trusted contact and manage the relationship through them; committee-level outreach typically stalls without that warm entry point.
