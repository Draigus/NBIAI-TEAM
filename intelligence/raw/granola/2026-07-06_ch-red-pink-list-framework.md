# Red and Pink List Framework for Incoming Studio Leaders

## Key Content

A structured onboarding tool for new senior hires and external advisors entering a chaotic or rapidly-scaled studio:

**Framework mechanics:**
- Red list: critical compliance or operational failures that must be addressed before anything else
- Pink list: structural gaps and process improvements that matter but can be sequenced
- Incoming leader builds their own list independently during their first 2-4 weeks
- Senior advisor cross-references: narrates which reds are already in motion and which are not yet started

**Value of the independent list:**
- Fresh-eyes perspective surfaces issues the existing team has normalised
- Cross-reference reveals whether the organisation is aware of its own failure points
- The delta between the two lists is diagnostic: overlap = confirmed risk; gap = what was missed
- New hire's observations feed into the senior advisor's remediation sequencing

**Observed scale:**
- Senior advisor's own red list on joining a ~55-person MMO studio was 81 items across production, art, and tech
- New senior hire (people ops) expected to produce a comparable list and share it in fortnightly 1:1s

**Probation window note:**
- UK unfair dismissal law: first four months are the critical window for structured performance testing
- Structured testing and clear performance resources must be established within probation, not retrospectively

## Decisions / Insights

- Glen decided: all senior hires asked to build a personal red list within first weeks and share it in regular 1:1s.
- Glen observed: 81 items identified on his own joining; new hire expected to surface comparable findings through a different lens.
- Glen concluded: cross-referencing independent lists is more diagnostic than briefing a new hire on existing problems -- the overlap confirms the real risks, the gaps reveal what was missed.

## Context

1:1 between senior advisor and a new people operations hire at a ~55-person MMO studio, 6 Jul 2026. First meeting; the senior advisor was sharing his working model and setting expectations for the new hire's onboarding contribution.

## Applicability

- Relevant when: onboarding a senior leader into a studio with known operational problems -- an independent red list prevents the new hire from inheriting the existing team's rationalisations.
- Relevant when: assessing whether a studio's leadership team knows its own failure points -- a new hire's independent list, when cross-referenced, reveals the delta between what is known and what is being tracked.
- Relevant when: acting as fractional CPO or senior advisor across multiple clients -- asking each incoming hire to build their own list produces structured intel without requiring a formal audit process.
- Relevant when: the first four months of any senior hire's tenure -- UK unfair dismissal law makes this the structured performance testing window; informal assessment is not sufficient protection.
