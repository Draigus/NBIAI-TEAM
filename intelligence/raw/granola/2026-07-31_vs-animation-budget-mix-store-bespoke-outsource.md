# VS Animation Budget: Store Baseline + Bespoke for Hero Characters + Outsource Partner

## Key Content

Vertical slice animation strategy for a small studio (~60 people) producing an MMO action-RPG:

**Tiered production model:**

- **Tier 1 (buy from store):** Generic NPCs, combat abilities, spells, skeleton (biped standard rig), sword-and-shield library. Purchase, retarget into game rig. Handles the majority of non-hero animation needs. Estimated cost: ~$350 for sword-and-shield baseline + skeleton pack.

- **Tier 2 (outsource partner + retarget):** Store-purchased animations retargeted by an outsource partner, then polished or used as-is by in-house animators. Reduces bespoke work to hero characters only.

- **Tier 3 (bespoke in-house or outsource):** Hero characters with novel rigs (e.g. floating-hand mechanic requiring 3-4 custom animations), major boss characters (2-3 bespoke animations each). Only characters where no commercially available animation works.

**Headcount assumption:** two mid-level animators can cover 4-5 fully custom builds within VS scope, given store + retarget handles the rest. Senior animator hire was not justified at this stage.

**Key check before purchasing:** verify commercial use license has no revenue cap clause. Some Unity/Unreal asset store packs cap revenue at which royalties apply.

**Outcome of tiered approach:** the majority of animation debt in a VS is covered by ~$350 in store assets; bespoke work is ring-fenced to the smallest possible set of hero characters.

## Decisions / Insights

- CPO decided: only fully bespoke characters (hero enemies with novel rigs) get bespoke animation budgets; everything else uses store assets + retarget
- CPO decided: two mid-level animators as the headcount for VS delivery -- senior not justified at this stage
- CPO noted: outsource partner to handle bespoke polish while permanent animators are hired -- prevents animation backlog blocking VS delivery
- Production rule: verify commercial use license (no revenue cap) before purchasing store animations

## Context

Solo production planning notes at a ~60-person UK/Cyprus MMO studio, July 2026, during VS1 animation scope planning. Studio targeting VS1 completion in September. Context: replacing animation team members and planning outsource coverage during hiring gap.
