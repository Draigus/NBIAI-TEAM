# Downtime Environment Art Pipeline: Marketplace Assets to Custom Kit Three-Step Plan

## Key Content

A core misalignment surfaced in the CH downtime environment build: no full custom art kit has ever been built for any of the downtime areas. All existing downtime assets are Marketplace purchases; Mythcore was partially custom but based on old workflows. The discovery creates both an investor data room risk (Marketplace licences surface in due diligence) and a pipeline quality gap.

Agreed direction: re-art the downtime environment using the existing layout. Do not rebuild or change the layout itself.

Three-step delivery plan:
1. Design blocks out social hub and tavern (Gary only, no kit-bashing), with a handoff deadline
2. Design locks the archway race route; anything off-route gets collision/blocking treatment
3. Environment team replaces all Marketplace assets with new custom kit assets, with a deadline

Division of ownership: Gary iterates blockout on social hub and tavern only. Michael's team owns all proxy kit and poly kit replacement. Gary has no kits to work with anyway; kit-bashing is a pipeline violation.

Outsourcing assets will accelerate the proxy kit build. Individual contractors ramp faster than studios; studios worth listing as backup. Art brief must come from concept, not AI; art director taking ownership of this going forward.

## Decisions / Insights

- Studio leadership decided: re-art downtime with existing layout; no rebuild, no layout change.
- Studio leadership decided: design blocks out social hub and tavern first; environment team replaces Marketplace assets third.
- Studio leadership decided: kit-bashing is a pipeline violation; Gary works only from blockout; no improvised assembly.
- Glen identified: Marketplace assets carry licences that surface in an investor data room at Series B scale; all such assets must be flagged and replaced before fundraise materials go live.
- Studio leadership decided: art brief originates from concept, not AI-generated references; art director owns this gate.

## Context

Executive Meeting at Couch Heroes, 14 Jul 2026. Attended by senior leadership, art director, design director, head of production, head of HR, EP, and Glen (CPO). The misalignment between environment team ownership and design blockout had not been resolved prior to this meeting; the three-step plan was agreed in the meeting itself.

## Applicability

Relevant when: a studio is preparing a vertical slice with environment art that has relied on Marketplace content -- each asset needs to be audited for licence terms before fundraise materials are assembled.
Relevant when: environment art ownership is unclear between a level design team and an art team -- a sequential plan (blockout ownership → route lock → asset replacement) with named owners eliminates overlap.
Relevant when: an art team has been using Marketplace content as permanent rather than placeholder -- the reclassification to placeholder triggers both a replacement backlog and an investor disclosure check.
Relevant when: an outsource plan needs to accelerate a proxy kit build -- individual contractors ramp faster than outsource studios; prioritise individual contractor list first.
