# AI-Assisted Concept Art Team Sizing: Five Artists to Two with 5x Output Expectation

## Key Content

When introducing AI concept tools into a game studio, the headcount model for a concept art team shifts significantly. A team of five is over-resourced at vertical slice stage when AI tooling is mandated.

**Revised model:**
- Target headcount: two artists (one senior generalist, one AI-proficient)
- Role accountability shifts from headcount-to-output to alignment-and-quality oversight
- New mandate framing: "Your job is to make sure the art style and levels are done right and it's aligned" -- not raw output volume
- Output expectation per artist: approximately 5x pre-AI baseline when proper tooling is adopted

**Transition approach:**
- Three-hour onboarding session per artist on required AI tools and courses
- Split responsibilities by output type (e.g., character art vs. environment art) even within a two-person team
- Keep the more versatile and technically capable artist; the unfocused or improvisation-prone artist is lower priority

**Selection criteria for the two retained:** strong versatility across character and environment; AI fluency is a hard requirement going forward.

## Decisions / Insights

- Studio leadership decided: concept art team reduced from five to two, with AI tools mandated to close the output gap
- Studio leadership observed: a 5x output multiplier per artist is the reasonable expectation when proper AI tools are adopted
- Studio leadership decided: team lead role shifts from managing raw output to ensuring art style coherence and level alignment

## Context

1:1 between embedded CPO and art leadership at a ~55-70 person game studio approaching vertical slice stage. 2026-07-24. Concept art team had been running at five artists, which both parties agreed was too large for current scope. Restructuring tied to approaching vertical slice and tighter scope constraints.

Relevant when: a studio is over-resourced in concept art relative to scope and budget -- two AI-enabled artists with a 5x output multiplier is the right-sizing heuristic.
