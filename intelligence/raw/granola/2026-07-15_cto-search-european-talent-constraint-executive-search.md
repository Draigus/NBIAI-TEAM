# CTO Search: European Talent Constraint and Executive Search Strategy Under Investment Deadline

## Key Content

A CTO search at a funded studio approaching an investment round faces a structural talent constraint in the European market that is distinct from the US market.

**European market constraint:**
- Microsoft's large-scale layoffs flooded the US games talent market; that supply did not translate to Europe
- Senior engineering leadership with multiplayer and large-scale MMO experience is genuinely scarce in Europe at CTO level
- Studios relying on European pipelines for this search face a structurally harder search than US counterparts

**Impact of approaching investment round on search strategy:**
- Walking into investor meetings without a named CTO weakens the studio's position in the round -- investors assess team completeness
- This pressure creates the temptation to hire below the bar or below the title to fill the gap
- Correct strategy: advance strong Technical Director-level candidates through the pipeline to keep them warm while CTO search continues; do not hire below CTO until the top role is filled
- Part-time senior advisors (ex-technical leadership with relevant domain experience) can bridge the gap without a hiring decision, particularly for high-level technical credibility in investor conversations

**Candidate compatibility assessment added to pipeline:**
- When a Creative Director and potential Technical Director are both being hired, assess compatibility between them as part of the pipeline -- not after both are onboarded
- Incompatibility between these two roles is a structural risk to the production; surface it before hiring decisions are made

## Decisions / Insights

- Studio CPO observed: European talent density at CTO level for multiplayer games is the structural constraint; this is not fixable by search effort alone.
- Studio CPO decided: advance Technical Director-level candidates through the pipeline to maintain engagement while the CTO search continues; do not hire below CTO until the top role is filled.
- Studio CPO decided: approach potential part-time senior technical advisors as a bridge during the CTO gap; framing as part-time advisory (not a role) removes compensation friction.
- Recruiter and CPO agreed: candidate compatibility between Creative Director and incoming Technical Director/CTO must be assessed as part of the pipeline before hire.

## Context

Weekly CTO search update between studio CPO and executive search firm (TrueSearch) at a ~70-person MMO studio, 15 Jul 2026. Search had been running for several months; two candidates who had looked like strong yeses withdrew. An investor round was approaching in late September. Studio had grown from 55 to 70 people during the search and was moving toward full production and vertical slice.

## Applicability

Relevant when: advising a European studio hiring senior engineering leadership -- set expectations that the European talent pool for multiplayer-specific CTO profiles is genuinely constrained; US search strategies and timelines do not translate directly.
Relevant when: a studio is approaching a funding round with a senior role still unfilled -- assess the investor signal risk of the gap; brief the investor narrative before the meeting rather than hoping the gap isn't noticed.
Relevant when: a studio is considering hiring below the bar to fill a gap before an investor round -- the risk of the wrong hire outweighs the credibility gap; structured part-time advisory bridges investor perception without a permanent hire.
Relevant when: a studio is hiring both a Creative Director and a Technical Director -- run a compatibility assessment before either offer is made; an incompatible pairing discovered post-onboarding is a production risk.
