# Game Studio Brand Identity: Pillars-First Build Sequence

## Key Content

Brand identity built ahead of game and studio pillars will fragment. Every iteration becomes driven by individual preference rather than strategy, producing materials that look unconnected and carry no shared logic. The correct sequence is:

**Brand build order (hard-sequenced):**
1. Game pillars ratified and locked (prerequisite; nothing else starts until this is done)
2. Studio brand investigation Q&A with leadership and key staff
3. Game brand investigation Q&A (separate from studio; focused on USPs, tone, product approach)
4. Synthesise learnings into draft brand direction
5. Competitive research informed by draft (not before -- research against a draft, not in a vacuum)
6. Full brand identity: brand bible, colour system, typography, character and asset briefs
7. Issue colour, character, and asset briefs to concept team

**Pitch decks and investor materials run in parallel as content-only:** do not apply art treatment until the brand is established. Narrative and data can be built without brand colours or visual identity.

**North star test:** within five seconds, an uninitiated person knows what the studio/game is and why they are here. If the five-second test fails, the brand has not done its job regardless of how polished the materials are.

**Artist vs. brand designer distinction:** artists optimise for visual quality; brand designers optimise for strategy and rules. Brand design cannot be delegated to artists without a brand designer owning the rules. When a studio lacks a brand designer, the output will be aesthetically varied but strategically incoherent.

## Decisions / Insights

- Studio CPO decided: nothing new gets made (no logos, no decks, no art treatments) until game pillars are locked; all brand cards marked as blocked on pillar ratification.
- Studio CPO and Brand Designer agreed: pitch decks proceed as content-only in parallel with brand work; art treatment waits for brand foundation.
- Studio CPO established: five-second uninitiated-person clarity test as the north star brand metric for all deliverables.
- Studio CPO observed: prior brand fragmentation (multiple logo iterations, conflicting styles coexisting, AI-generated variants circulating externally) is the result of building on "what the founder wants" with no research, strategy, or rules behind it.

## Context

Kickoff meeting between CPO and Brand Designer at a ~70-person MMO studio, 15 Jul 2026. Studio brand was severely fragmented: multiple logo iterations, conflicting visual styles across materials, outdated pitch deck roadmap, game assets insufficient for marketing. A dedicated Brand workstream had just been created, with the first task being a Brand Investigation Q&A with leadership. Pitching to investors was a near-term requirement with a September deadline.

## Applicability

Relevant when: advising a studio on brand strategy before an investment round -- enforce the pillars-first sequence; brand materials built without locked pillars will need to be rebuilt when pillars change.
Relevant when: a studio's marketing materials look inconsistent across investor deck, social, and game assets -- diagnose whether a brand bible exists and whether it predates or follows pillar lock.
Relevant when: a studio assigns brand design work to artists or engineers by default -- flag the artist/brand-designer distinction and assess whether a dedicated brand designer is in the team.
Relevant when: a studio is preparing investor materials under time pressure -- separate content (narrative, data, structure) from art treatment; content can proceed immediately, art treatment must wait for brand foundation.
