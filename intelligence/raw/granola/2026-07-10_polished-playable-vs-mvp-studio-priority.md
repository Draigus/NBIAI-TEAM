# "Polished Playable" vs MVP: Studio Priority Framework and C-Level-First Leadership Training

## Key Content

A ~55-person MMO studio replaced "MVP" language with "polished playable" and adopted a C-level-first leadership development sequence.

**Polished playable framing:**
- "MVP" dropped due to industry association with half-finished shipped titles
- "Polished playable" sets the expected quality bar for the vertical slice deliverable
- Studio's first VS expected to feel boring internally -- framed explicitly as the fuel to rally the team, not a failure signal
- No redesigns or new feature ideation during vertical slice; all new ideas routed to a designated holding document, categorised as locked/unlocked

**C-level-first leadership training sequence:**
- Do NOT teach leads/managers until C-level expectations are defined first
- Proposed training structure: half to full day; define expectations at C-level → teach leading, directing, managing within agile framework in that order
- User stories must be written to a specific, agreed Definition of Done before training is considered complete
- Rationale: cascading agile training before alignment at the top creates inconsistent implementation below

**Studio game simulator concept:**
- First VS framed not as the product but as a diagnostic: "building a game simulator to expose what the studio can and can't do"
- Leadership advised not to present it as "almost the game" -- the boring-first expectation must be set in advance

## Decisions / Insights

- Studio CPO decided: "polished playable" replaces MVP as the standard language for vertical slice output.
- Studio CPO decided: design freeze in place until VS is in-game; new ideas go to a locked document, not into sprint.
- Studio CPO decided: leadership and agile training starts at C-level alignment before cascading to managers.
- Studio CPO observed: the first VS will feel boring, and this should be pre-communicated as a known outcome to prevent misread as failure.

## Context

Informal leadership sync at a ~55-person MMO studio between CPO and Executive Producer, July 2026. Studio in active vertical slice production with 3-4 months of structured production leadership in place.

## Applicability

- Relevant when: a studio is using MVP language that is eroding internal or external quality expectations -- reframe as "polished playable" and define what that means explicitly.
- Relevant when: a studio is planning leadership or agile training -- start with C-level alignment before cascading; training without top-down clarity produces inconsistent results.
- Relevant when: a studio's vertical slice is in progress and new design ideas keep surfacing -- implement a locked/unlocked holding document with a clear no-redesign rule for the VS window.
- Relevant when: a studio's first playable is expected to feel rough -- pre-communicate "boring is the expected outcome" to prevent the team from reading it as failure and losing confidence.
- Relevant when: NBI is advising on VS milestone framing -- the "game simulator" diagnostic framing is useful for resetting expectations about first-pass quality.
