# NBI AI Readiness Framework: Four-Phase Model

## Key Content

Glen's framework for AI readiness engagements, applicable to any organisation seeking to adopt AI operationally. Validated through approximately 300 audits by a collaborator (Brad) and now being positioned as an NBI service offering.

**Four phases:**

1. **Know your business** -- Document in detail what the business actually does, how it works, where the decisions are made, and where the knowledge lives. Most organisations cannot answer this before AI is introduced.

2. **Define AI value** -- Map repetitive and research workflows. Identify where AI creates leverage (time compression, quality uplift, cost reduction). Produces a prioritised opportunity map.

3. **Structure AI for success** -- Architect the AI operating system. Define how AI tools sit within workflows, who owns them, how outputs are quality-controlled. Governance, tooling selection, and process integration.

4. **Prepare the organisation** -- Train staff to be AI-native. Not tool training (how to use ChatGPT) but operating model training (how to think as an originator and vet, with AI as partner and red-teamer).

**Core philosophy:**
- Employee as originator and vet; AI as partner and red-teamer
- AI-over-employee model (e.g. Babylon Health) is a documented failure pattern -- do not recommend
- Progression path: AI as tool → AI as advisor → AI as partner → AI as revenue generator
- NBI's own usage as a live demo: AI monitors workloads, flags overloads, surfaces missed opportunities, drafts keep-warm emails, reads meeting notes for cross-client opportunities
