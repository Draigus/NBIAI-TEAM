# Systems Designer Role Definition for MMO: Two Sub-Types and the Pillar-Lock Gate

## Key Content

A framework for defining and hiring a systems designer at an MMO studio, including two distinct sub-types and a critical prerequisite gate.

**The "architect mechanic" definition:**
- Systems designer blueprints systems on paper and tunes variables; does not write code
- Works in Excel/database tools; pairs with an engineer for implementation
- Scope: guild system, auction house, inventory, progression, career pathing
- Distinct from a level designer (spatial), economy designer (numbers), or engineer (implementation)

**Two sub-types (distinct hires):**
1. Gameplay systems designer: auction house, guild, inventory, traditional metagame systems; more familiar archetype; multiple candidates exist in the MMO market
2. World/emergence systems designer: dynamic world, corruption spread, day/night cycles, emergent NPC behaviour, world-state-driven gameplay; rarer; the differentiator vs. traditional MMOs

**Which to hire first:**
- World/emergence systems is the bigger creative gap for an MMO aiming at differentiation
- Gameplay systems (traditional) is easier to hire and lower risk if world systems vision is unclear
- Senior studio advisor position: do not hire either until game pillars are locked
   - With 5+ conflicting systemic visions in the studio, hiring to a spec that doesn't yet exist will produce misaligned work
   - Pillar alignment comes first; systems designer hire follows from the locked pillar set

**Candidate profile:**
- Multiple MMO systems credits (not just single-player or shooter)
- Metagame thinking native (guild systems, progression ladders, auction house dynamics)
- Excel-native for variable tuning and balance modelling
- Example profile: 23 years experience across EverQuest 2, Guild Wars 2, Diablo 4, live-service MMO

**Priority sequencing:**
- Priority 1: UI/UX designer (biggest current gap) + narrative designer (MMO background preferred)
- Systems designer: next wave, after pillars are locked
- Economy/balance designers: mid-production hires, not urgent in pre-production

## Decisions / Insights

- Studio advisor decided: systems designer hire is blocked until game pillars are locked -- multiple conflicting systemic visions in the studio make the hire premature.
- Studio advisor concluded: world/emergence systems is the bigger differentiator gap for an MMO aiming at dynamic world design; gameplay systems is lower risk but less differentiating.
- Studio advisor observed: the "architect mechanic" framing -- blueprints systems, tunes in Excel, pairs with engineer -- is the correct job description anchor for this role in MMO production.
- Studio leadership aligned: priority 1 hires are UI/UX and narrative designer; systems designer follows from pillar lock.
