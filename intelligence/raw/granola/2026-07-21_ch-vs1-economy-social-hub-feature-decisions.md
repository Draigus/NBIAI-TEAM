# CH VS1: Economy, Social Hub, and Core Feature Decisions

## Key Content

**Economy and currency (VS1 non-negotiables):**
- Currency unit: "bits," denominated in hundreds (not thousands). Marble-style visual concept (bronze/silver/gold-red), tied to Source energy lore. RGB color coding as in-universe reference. "Bits/kilobits/megabits" rejected as too on-the-nose.
- In-game store: VS1 = simple buy/sell (e.g., 20 bits for a sword). VS3 = full tiered pricing.
- Full gains/drains economic system: deferred to VS3.

**Bazaar replaces auction house:**
- Decision: replace single auction house building with a bazaar district (categorical stalls: weapons, armor, clothing, materials). Players list items from home; items route to relevant stall automatically.
- Rationale: reduces render load (Iron Forge problem), simpler for new players, creates foot traffic and NPC atmosphere, partner asset opportunity.
- Bank (storage) still required as VS1 non-negotiable; mostly built already.
- Full player-to-player trading: VS3/VS4.

**Double jump removed; ledge grab instead:**
- Decision: double jump removed; replaced by ledge grab + recovery.
- Rationale: double jump breaks immersion for armored/tank characters.
- Constraint: ledge grab must be fluid and fast, not slow like Uncharted.
- Target: ledge grab in place within 5 months (before VS1 ship).
- Robin to verify level design gaps do not require double jump in the interim.
- Communication: full team alignment on removal by end of week; Panos and Seth flagged as the two designers with strong opinions.

**Guild system:**
- Guild house: VS1 non-negotiable; UI mock-ups required even without back-end systems.
- Tagging system (e.g., "gathering guild"): creative direction only, not required for VS1.
- Retention framing: guilds increase retention ~75% in this genre.

**UX/UI hire:** Two finalists -- Matt and Alexandra (Assassin's Creed background, scripting skills). Glen meets both before decision.

## Decisions / Insights

- Glen, Vardis, Robin decided: bazaar district replaces single auction house; direction pursued.
- Glen decided: double jump killed; ledge grab is the replacement; must be fast/fluid.
- Glen decided: guild UI mock-ups required for VS1 even without back-end (investor needs visual context).
- Pattern: phasing economy complexity (simple store VS1 → full bazaar VS3) allows the social hub to be functional for investors without the full economic simulation.
