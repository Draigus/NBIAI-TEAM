# Staged Challenge Sequencing as a Leadership Development Tool

## Key Content

Core principle: a self-discovered truth carries more weight than one given to you. When developing a defensive or insecure leader, the most effective approach is to engineer the challenging moments rather than wait for them to occur organically.

Development arc pattern observed: denial and insecurity → do the scary thing → it goes okay → confidence starts building. Each success must be reinforced before the next harder test is introduced.

Three-stage challenge sequencing (ascending difficulty):
1. Advisor/CPO as first hurdle -- controlled, safe to fail, recoverable
2. Peer who naturally encroaches on the leader's territory -- real friction, predictable timing
3. Opinionated senior colleague -- natural friction, no need to manufacture

A key milestone indicator is vulnerability: when the leader can say "I don't know how to do this" without prompting, the defensive phase is over. Until then, pace challenges accordingly.

Active management of the encroacher: give them open territory (AI tooling, explorations) so they are not penned in with the leader-in-development -- reduces friction during the fragile growth phase.

- Glen concluded: manufacture a stumble before a real one arrives -- structure it so the leader fights, negotiates down, wins partial ground, builds the muscle without the full consequence.
