# UK Game Studio Contractor Compliance: Common Failure Points at Scaling Studios

## Key Content

A ~55-person UK/Cyprus game studio joining a senior advisor's oversight uncovered 6-7 active compliance failures within the first weeks. Common failure modes in UK game studios that have scaled rapidly without building HR infrastructure:

**Contractor misclassification (highest risk):**
- Offering contractors sick pay, bereavement leave, or vacation = £60,000 fine per infraction
- This is the single highest-exposure item at rapidly-scaled studios; multiple infractions can be active simultaneously
- Right-to-work checks required for UK contractors, not only for FTE; frequently missed

**Immigration and sponsorship:**
- Certificate of sponsorship: HMRC and Home Office audit thoroughly before granting; documentation must be complete before the application, not in parallel
- Studios sponsoring international hires need right-to-work infrastructure in place before application, not assembled during it

**Corporate banking:**
- Some fintech business banks are not accepted by financial counterparties in regulated UK payroll and compliance contexts; traditional business banking is required

**Remediation approach:**
- Fix compliance quietly before attracting regulatory attention ("don't poke the bear")
- Treat each infraction category as a separate workstream with a named owner
- Design the data/document pipeline first; instruct tooling to route documents correctly afterwards

## Decisions / Insights

- Glen observed: contractor misclassification (vacation/sick pay offered to contractors) was the highest-exposure item; £60k per infraction, multiple infractions active on joining.
- Glen decided: fix compliance quietly -- principle is not to attract regulatory attention while remediating.
- Glen identified: right-to-work checks for UK contractors are routinely missed; studios assume they apply only to FTE.
- Glen concluded: design the document and data pipeline first, then configure tooling; not the reverse.

## Context

1:1 between senior advisor and a new people operations hire at a ~55-person UK/Cyprus MMO studio, 6 Jul 2026. The studio had grown rapidly from approximately 30 to 55 staff without building a compliance infrastructure to match; multiple infractions were being remediated concurrently.

## Applicability

- Relevant when: onboarding as a fractional advisor at a UK game studio that has grown from startup to 30-80 staff -- rapid scaling almost always outpaces HR infrastructure.
- Relevant when: a UK studio is offering any benefits to contractors -- vacation, sick pay, or bereavement leave offered to a contractor is a misclassification exposure at £60k per infraction.
- Relevant when: a studio is hiring international staff requiring sponsorship -- right-to-work infrastructure and compliant banking must be in place before the application, not during it.
- Relevant when: advising a studio on banking for payroll or compliance purposes -- verify the chosen bank is accepted in the studio's regulatory context; fintech banks may not be.
