# Studio Ideation Silos: Shadow Conversations and the Ideation Channel Fix

## Key Content

A pattern common in founder-led studios: the founder or CEO privately shares ideas with each lead separately, creating "shadow conversations." Each lead receives different information. Nobody knows who else was consulted. The same topic is being discussed in parallel threads that never converge.

**Why this is damaging:**
- Leads make decisions or form positions based on incomplete context
- The founder believes alignment has been reached when only individual conversations happened
- Cross-functional ideas received privately tend to stay private, limiting broader input
- Leads end up contradicting each other in group settings because they received different briefs

**The fix: a dedicated ideation channel with a mandatory routing rule**
- Create a shared ideation channel (Slack, Teams, Discord -- channel is the medium, not the solution)
- Rule: any lead who receives a private idea from a founder or executive is expected to post it in the ideation channel
- The lead posts the idea, not the founder -- this maintains the founder's relationship while surfacing the content
- The channel becomes the clearing house; private idea threads get surfaced and cross-pollinated
- The game director (or equivalent coordination role) is responsible for asking the founder directly to route new ideas into the channel rather than sending them bilaterally

**What this doesn't fix:** a founder who consults leads individually on sensitive HR or personnel matters -- that stays bilateral. The fix is for creative, scope, and strategic ideas only.

## Decisions / Insights

- Studio game director decided: to introduce a dedicated ideation channel and ask the founder directly to route creative ideas there rather than sending privately
- Studio game director observed: the silo pattern emerges when a founder has high creative energy but no structured channel for it -- the individual conversations are not malicious, they are habitual
- Studio leadership observed: the fix must come from the game director asking the founder to route through the channel -- not from posting on the founder's behalf (that creates a different trust problem)

## Context

1:1 between embedded CPO and game director at a ~55-70 person MMO studio. 2026-07-24. Founder-CEO observed to be sharing creative ideas with each department lead separately. Different leads receiving different versions of evolving ideas, generating misaligned decisions and frustration in group settings.

## Applicability

Relevant when: a founder-led studio is showing misalignment in group leadership sessions -- the "shadow conversation" silo pattern is a common root cause.
Relevant when: advising a game director or EP on managing a high-energy creative founder -- the ideation channel is the structural fix that respects the founder's style while creating transparency.
Relevant when: setting up communication infrastructure at a new client studio -- the ideation channel is an early quick win that prevents escalating misalignment.
Relevant when: a studio has multiple leads who regularly express surprise at what the founder told other leads -- this is the diagnostic symptom of the silo pattern.
