# CH Shortal Peak Layout: Corruption Aesthetic and Boss Room Design Decisions

## Key Content

World and level design review for the Shortal Peak area of CH's MMO vertical slice.

**Layout decisions:**
- Multi-layer layout, vista views, and cliffside journey all approved; area scale deemed appropriate
- Tight, linear onboarding is intentional for the first player experience -- explorable spaces can be visually teased without being built
- Gray-boxing considered complete enough to proceed

**Corruption narrative visual language:**
- Ancient mythcore architecture that has been defiled and "goblinized" -- Minas Morgul / Gondorian aesthetic overlaid with goblin clamps, metal, and wood
- Corruption must be present but restrained; emergent, not overwhelming
- Contrast of former beauty vs. current defilement is the central visual story
- Interior: goblins mining crystals, feeding corruption into a central cauldron/dais; crystals in carts and spilled outward
- Cult graffiti as subtle tease in ruins -- no actual cultists in VS

**Player intent in VS:**
- Player is not meant to win this area in VS1; objective is reconnaissance and partial corruption cleanup
- Guardian appears; player escapes via glider to camp, returns later with better gear

**Boss room and tower scope:**
- Tower needs to be roughly twice as wide as current; guardian must be large enough to punch through it
- Interior flow: main room (guardian), elevator partway up, degraded staircase with improvised exterior gantries, summit library (signals Digit One history)
- **Rule:** white-box boss room and top library first; let those two spaces dictate all proportions below them

## Decisions / Insights

- Glen and leads decided: white-box the two key interaction spaces (boss room, summit library) before designing entryways or determining overall scale -- anchor spaces dictate proportions, not the reverse.
- Design leads decided: tight linear onboarding is a design feature for VS1, not a flaw; exploration is teased visually without being fully constructed.
- Design leads decided: corruption aesthetic must be restrained -- beauty-vs-defilement contrast requires the original architecture to remain legible beneath the corruption layer.
- Glen decided: player objective in Shortal Peak VS is reconnaissance and partial cleanup; full clear happens in a return visit with upgraded gear.

## Context

World and level design review session, Jun 30 2026, attended by Glen, Robin Jubber (art lead), Seth Dahl (level design), David Luong (art direction), Vardis (CEO), Gary Platner (level design lead), and Michael Dunnam (design director). Reviewed the Shortal Peak area layout and established aesthetic and structural decisions for the VS1 build.

## Applicability

- Relevant when: designing a tutorial or onboarding area for an MMO -- tight linearity is acceptable and preferable for the first pass; exploration appetite can be signalled visually before the space is built.
- Relevant when: establishing a corruption or defilement aesthetic for an environment -- the restraint principle (contrast preserved, beauty legible beneath decay) avoids visual noise and preserves the narrative read.
- Relevant when: scoping a boss encounter area for a vertical slice -- white-box the key interaction space first, then let it dictate surrounding proportions rather than designing inward from the perimeter.
- Relevant when: sequencing an encounter for a vertical slice where the player is out-powered -- reconnaissance + partial objective + escape creates a satisfying defeat without frustrating the player.
