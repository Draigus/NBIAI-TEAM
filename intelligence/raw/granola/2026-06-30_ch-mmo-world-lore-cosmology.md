# CH MMO: World Lore, Cosmology, and Narrative Structure (Confirmed)

## Key Content

Full lore session with CEO, Game Director, key leads and designers confirming the canonical world design for CH's MMO.

**Cosmology:**
- Digit One: first human to digitalise himself; created Darwin (world engine), the Cromas (digital beings), and Drisden (failed prototype, now antagonist). Disappeared through Portal Peak. Intentionally enigmatic -- factions form their own theories.
- The Ardents: four surviving loyal Cromas -- Sara (creation/life), Nero (data integrity), Agni (defence), Merivia (information/transport)
- The Fracturing: Drisden exploited a digital empire collapse; Digit One fractured the universe to stop him (~98% casualty rate); Luma sacrificed herself to signal survivors; civilisation rebuilt on her shard

**Drisden (antagonist model):**
- Pre-dates the Cromas; never directly confronted by players ("Satan" framing -- always background, never a one-on-one boss)
- Keeps stakes consistent across all expansions without a new villain per DLC
- Corruption stages: wilting/glitching environment → corrupted NPCs joining cults → full black-matter takeover → consumption
- Forces: the Shifted (powerful AI constructs), the Faulty (corrupted NPCs), the Leviathan (cult on Digi-Isle, first main threat)

**Magic system (glyphs, not runes):**
- 64-symbol proto-language tied thematically to coding language
- Symbols combine to produce spells or communicate complex ideas
- Players discover magic progressively -- not available from level 1
- Full mage build signals genuine player investment
- Spell combinations are predetermined (crafting-system model), not free-form

**Factions (Hogwarts model -- cooperative, not competitive):**
- Weavers (Sara): creators, healers, aesthetics players
- Wardens: killers, tanks, DPS, competitive players
- Keepers: min-maxers, guild leaders, strategy players
- Seekers: collectors, market players, traders
- Players expected to move between factions; faction quest givers telegraph type by colour/aesthetic

**Visual eras:**
- Mythcore: ancient golden era, grand impossible architecture, ruins
- Gridcore: post-Fracturing present, cobbled-together civilisation, degraded tech
- Rule: mythcore density decreases with distance from original centre; outskirts are almost fantasy-village with one mythcore token

**Narrative production model:**
- Long arc with 2-chapter micro-arcs per expansion (not 18-month WoW-style reinventions)
- Breadcrumb model: each region teases the next two
- Expansion size scoped to team velocity (20 or 50 hours depending on capability)
- VS1 is systems-first, not story-heavy; narrative hooks added in VS2+

**Open mysteries kept deliberately unresolved:** who was Digit One on Earth and where did he go; why did the portal reactivate now; what is Drisden's true nature; how many glyphs exist (5-6 at launch, more added per update).

## Decisions / Insights

- CEO and Game Director confirmed: Drisden is the permanent antagonist across all expansions, never a direct boss encounter -- prevents antagonist fatigue and keeps stakes scalable.
- Design team confirmed: magic system uses glyphs (not runes), 64-symbol proto-language, progression-unlocked, predetermined combinations only.
- Design team confirmed: factions map to player psychology archetypes (Hogwarts model) and are cooperative not competitive -- players move between factions freely.
- Art leads confirmed: mythcore and gridcore are the two core visual eras; density gradient from centre outward governs which style dominates each zone.
- Production confirmed: VS1 proves core game loop only; narrative layering deferred to VS2+.
