# MMO Platform Design: Cross-Studio Rewards as Discovery and Community Bridge

## Key Content

The studio's MMO is designed as a retention and social hub, not a shallow metaverse. The platform layer connects the MMO to partner studios: players earn in-world rewards (wearables, housing items, marketplace goods) by completing achievements in external partner games.

**Cross-studio reward mechanic as stated:**
- A player completes a themed campaign in a partner title (e.g. a historical strategy game campaign as a named faction leader)
- The corresponding in-world reward (e.g. the faction leader's iconic item) becomes available in the MMO
- Items can be: equipped on character, displayed in player housing, or traded on the marketplace
- The reward creates a reason for players of the partner title to try the MMO; creates a reason for MMO players to try the partner title

**Core design word: discovery**
- The platform is designed to help players discover new friends, new games, and new social groups through the shared world
- Social graph expands naturally through the cross-studio mechanic -- a FIFA player and a strategy game player may meet in the MMO world before they have any other shared context
- Discord and other social layers are treated as complementary, not as competition

**Platform vs. game UI split:**
- The launcher/platform side maintains a clean, accessible UI (onboarding and navigation for the broader ecosystem)
- The game UI inside the MMO has more creative latitude for innovation and genre expression
- The two layers have deliberately different design registers to match their different user contexts

## Decisions / Insights

- Studio leadership stated: the MMO is the retention vehicle and social core -- the platform mechanic is the bridge that brings players from partner games into the shared world
- Studio leadership stated: discovery (of friends, games, social groups) is the core design word -- every platform mechanic should be evaluated against whether it advances discovery
- Studio leadership stated: Discord and social apps are complements, not threats -- the platform does not try to replace them but rather amplifies their function within the shared world

## Context

Studio leadership (CEO) briefing a senior UI/UX practitioner joining the team, 2026-07-27. The briefing was given to align a new senior hire with the platform vision before they begin scoping UI systems.

## Applicability

Relevant when: advising on platform strategy for an MMO with a social hub ambition -- the cross-studio reward mechanic is a proven concept for bridging player communities without requiring players to abandon their existing games.
Relevant when: scoping marketplace design for an MMO -- items earned through external game achievements create supply-side diversity without requiring the studio to generate all reward content internally.
Relevant when: evaluating the CH platform vision against other metaverse or cross-title platform plays -- the discovery-first framing distinguishes it from ownership-first (NFT/Web3) approaches.
Relevant when: designing the platform UX -- maintaining a clean, accessible launcher layer separate from the genre-expressive game UI is a structural decision that affects onboarding for both MMO players and partner-game players arriving cold.
