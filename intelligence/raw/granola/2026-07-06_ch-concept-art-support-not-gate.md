# Concept Art as Support Function, Not Pipeline Gate

## Key Content

A studio was experiencing friction between concept and environment art teams because concept had positioned itself as a gating authority: requiring sign-off before environment artists could build.

**Position established:**
- Concept art is a support function that feeds environment and character art; it does not own final approval
- Assets already built and approved by the relevant lead or director are not sent back to concept, regardless of whether concept was involved in their creation
- AI art for ideation is acceptable provided the approach is aligned in advance; retrospective objection is not valid once assets are approved
- Concept team redirected to new briefs in parallel; no production pause for retrospective sign-off

**AI art policy gap as root cause:**
- Studio had no formal AI art policy; this is the structural root of the pipeline friction
- Concept team had begun guard-dogging because they felt cut out of the process, not because of a genuine quality control mandate
- Policy conversation scheduled with Art Director before any further guidance issued to teams
- Interim: AI ideation is not blocked, but must be aligned before it informs production assets

## Decisions / Insights

- Glen decided: concept art is a support function; no rollback on approved assets regardless of concept involvement in their creation.
- Glen decided: concept team redirected to new work in parallel; no production pause pending retrospective sign-off.
- Glen identified: the absence of an AI art policy is the root cause of pipeline misalignment; policy conversation required before issuing further team guidance.
- Glen observed: concept guard-dogging is a symptom of feeling excluded earlier in the process, not a genuine quality control response.

## Context

Tutorial Cave kick-off at a ~55-person MMO studio, 6 Jul 2026. Art pipeline friction had emerged between concept and environment art teams, with concept self-organising as a gating authority. The senior advisor intervened to clarify the support model and schedule an AI policy conversation.

## Applicability

- Relevant when: concept art has self-organised into a gatekeeping role -- the diagnostic is whether environment art is paused waiting for concept sign-off rather than building.
- Relevant when: a studio lacks an AI art policy -- friction over AI-generated reference material emerges as soon as any team uses it without prior alignment, regardless of output quality.
- Relevant when: assets are already built and approved by relevant leads -- retrospective concept sign-off is not a valid pipeline requirement; it adds friction without improving the asset.
- Relevant when: a creative team is guard-dogging -- the root cause is usually exclusion earlier in the process, not a genuine quality control mandate; the fix is clearer early briefs, not more gate control.
