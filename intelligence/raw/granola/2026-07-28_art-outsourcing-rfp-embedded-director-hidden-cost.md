# Art Outsourcing RFP: Embedded Art Director as Hidden Cost and Correct RFP Process

## Key Content

A recurring error in vendor outsourcing quotes: vendors include an embedded art director in their cost model, which the client does not catch until after quotes are reviewed. This inflates the comparable against headcount because the studio already has art direction capacity internally.

**Error pattern:** Studio commissions outsourcing quotes for environment art. Vendor includes an art director in the cost (to manage their own team). Studio compares the total quote against headcount costs without adjusting for the art director component. The comparison overstates the cost of outsourcing relative to doing the work internally.

**Correct RFP process (sequential):**
1. Build a proper brief first -- scope, asset list, quality bar with visual references
2. Get NDA signed with each vendor before sharing brief
3. Send brief to vendors; vendors slice the work into costs themselves
4. Compare vendor costs against equivalent headcount cost -- but strip any embedded AD cost from the vendor quote before comparing

**Why brief-first matters:** vendors cannot price accurately without a brief. Without it, they quote for their standard offering, not for the studio's actual needs. Asking for costs before the brief produces comparison-grade data only if the brief is later provided.

## Decisions / Insights

- Studio CPO identified: outsourcing quote included an embedded art director that was not caught until after review; this distorts the cost comparison against headcount
- Studio CPO directed: remove the art director from the quote, build a proper brief, get NDA signed, then re-bid
- Studio CPO stated: correct RFP sequence is brief first, NDA, then vendor cost-slicing -- not request-for-price first

## Context

Art outsourcing review at a ~55-70 person MMO studio, July 2026. Multiple vendors had submitted environment art quotes. One quote included an embedded art director that the studio had not requested and did not need. The error was caught during a production review, not during quote review.

## Applicability

Relevant when: reviewing outsourcing quotes from art vendors -- always audit whether an art director or equivalent supervisory role is embedded in the cost before comparing to headcount.
Relevant when: commissioning outsourcing quotes -- provide a brief and require NDA signature before vendors price the work; quotes without a brief are estimate-grade, not comparison-grade.
Relevant when: advising a studio on outsourcing cost-benefit analysis -- the comparison baseline is headcount cost for equivalent output, not headline quote cost.
Relevant when: a studio is in the early stages of outsourcing for the first time -- the embedded-director error is common and is easy to miss when reviewing multiple vendor quotes simultaneously.
