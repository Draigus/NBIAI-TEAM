# CTO vs Technical Director: Behavioural Distinction and Hiring Implications

## Key Content

CTO and TD are frequently conflated in game studios. Operationally they require different people:

**CTO profile:**
- Sets architecture direction for the studio
- Represents engineering in investor meetings and to external stakeholders
- Coaches the team against over-engineering; manages the balance of debt vs. velocity
- Builds and manages the engineering organisation; thinks in headcount and org design

**Technical Director profile:**
- Closer to the code; rolls up sleeves alongside engineers
- Teaches by writing code and showing, not by explaining in English
- Drives toward deadlines; owns delivery accountability within the engineering team
- Manages individual contributor growth directly

**Practical implications:**
- A strong TD with CTO ambitions is different from a CTO -- do not confuse willingness for capability
- Hiring two TDs (roughly front-end and back-end split) and letting their skills define the actual boundary is a valid approach for an MMO studio that needs both investor-facing leadership and hands-on delivery
- Org chart at hiring is an intention; the people hired define the actual reality of the roles
- A candidate comfortable with a TD title after interviewing for CTO is a signal of self-awareness, not failure

## Decisions / Insights

- Architect concluded: CTO and TD are distinct roles requiring different profiles; conflating them leads to miscast hires.
- Architect recommended: for MMO studios at the 50-100 person scale, hiring two TDs with loosely defined boundaries (front-end / back-end) and letting capability shape the actual split is more effective than one CTO doing both.
- Architect observed: a candidate who self-selects to TD after a CTO interview process is demonstrating self-awareness about their own profile, not a downgrade.
- Architect observed: the org chart as drawn is always an intention; the people hired always reshape it.

## Context

Technical interview with a candidate for a senior engineering role at a ~55-person MMO studio, 7 Jul 2026. Candidate had significant head-of-engineering experience and correctly self-identified as a closer fit to TD than CTO during the session. Discussion was prompted by the studio's uncertainty about whether it needed a CTO or TD.

## Applicability

- Relevant when: a game studio is hiring its first senior technical leader and conflating CTO and TD titles -- the diagnostic question is whether they need someone investor-facing or someone who codes alongside the team.
- Relevant when: advising on an engineering org structure for an MMO at the 50-100 person scale -- two TDs with fuzzy but complementary scope often works better than one CTO.
- Relevant when: a CTO candidate self-selects to TD during the process -- treat this as a positive data point about self-awareness, not a failure of the interview or the candidate.
- Relevant when: a studio's org chart does not match operational reality -- the people in the roles define the roles, regardless of what the chart says.
