# Studio Operations Leadership Model: Nebulous to Controlled Chaos

## Key Content

Glen's operational leadership model for embedded advisory roles: move from nebulous (no shared understanding of state) to chaos (activity is happening but uncoordinated) to controlled chaos (activity is coordinated and tracked) -- then step back from operations.

The goal of embedding an operations lead (finance, HR, or general ops) is not execution but a "second set of lenses on the business." They flag concerns, challenge decisions, and surface blind spots -- not just complete tasks.

Glen's explicit concern: no one currently challenges his decisions, which he identifies as a personal risk. He deliberately structured the ops lead role to provide direct pushback, framing it as a requirement of the role rather than optional.

Single source of truth is a persistent gap in early-stage studios. Folders and shared drives become dumping grounds, not sources of truth. A project management tool with enforced structure is the intended fix.

Hiring philosophy: hire for 18-month needs, not immediate needs. A TA function is only justified when hiring volume is high enough to define a per-month target.

## Decisions / Insights

- Glen concluded: the value of an operations hire is as a challenger and second lens, not as an executor -- an ops hire who only executes is underutilised
- Glen observed: unchallenged decision-making is a personal risk he actively manages by structuring roles to provide pushback
- Glen advised operations lead: pick top three priorities, finish one at a time -- over-commitment and under-delivery is a common failure mode for ops generalists
- Pattern identified: studios consistently under-justify TA hires because they cannot name a monthly hiring volume that would make the hire cost-effective

## Context

NBI advisory engagement with a 55-70 person game studio. Glen working with a new finance/operations lead embedded at the client. Discussion of role framing, working relationship, and organisational maturity model. 2026-07-22.
