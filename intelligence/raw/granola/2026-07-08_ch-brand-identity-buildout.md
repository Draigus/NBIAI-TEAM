# CH Brand Identity: Current State and Buildout Priority Sequence

## Key Content

Brand audit and plan agreed with CH's graphic designer/social media lead, 8 Jul 2026.

**Current state (problems):**
- No consistent brand across website, presentations, logo, and game art
- Logo has retro/arcade aesthetic; game art does not match it
- Brand colour (purple) was chosen for a personal reason, not a strategic one
- Website took six months to build and does not reflect the studio's personality
- Concept art assets look like assets from multiple different games
- LinkedIn used only for recruiting posts; no brand or studio culture content
- Discord paused: no content, no campaign cadence to sustain a beat

**Priority sequence agreed:**
1. Brand identity: colour, tone, visual language
2. Brand persona: voice, dark humour, quippish tone
3. Brand Bible: locks identity and persona for all teams (including incoming UI designer)
4. Web presence (after Bible): website rebuild, LinkedIn content strategy
5. Player segmentation: existing audience work is internal/exploratory; full market segment study needed
6. Community management plan: build the plan now; do not execute until there is gameplay and a content cadence

**Key decisions:**
- Larisa will work directly with CPO on brand; continues reporting to Art Director but CPO directs her work
- Community management: plan built now; execution deferred until sufficient gameplay content exists
- Concept artists at ~35% capacity available to produce assets for brand briefs
- Reference quality benchmark: old Blizzard website as the visual identity standard

**Next step:** brand and platform walkthrough with CPO, Tuesday 14 Jul 2026, 2--3pm (Larisa to prepare historical brand materials, community plan, pitch decks, and platform walkthrough).
