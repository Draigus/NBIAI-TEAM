# Studio Leadership Three-Tier Meeting Hierarchy with RYG Department Status

## Key Content

Three-tier meeting structure for a 55-70 person game studio operating under an embedded CPO:

**Tier 1 -- C-Level:** Glen only. Strategic direction and decision authority, no operational agenda items.

**Tier 2 -- Studio Leadership Team (SLT):** Department heads. Format: per-department RYG status view. Each department reports top 3 priorities, owner, due date, and dependencies.
- Operations side (COO): HR, Finance, IT, Legal
- Product side (CPO): Art, Design, Game

**Tier 3 -- Leads:** Run by production, not by executive leadership. Operational coordination below SLT.

Implementation pattern: delegate template building and update-chasing to a producer/coordinator. Give the meeting owner (e.g., EP) ownership of running the meeting, not building it. The coordinator chases updates; the EP runs the session.

Worklist tool alongside meetings: a priority-scored Excel with aggregate scores, department codes, and hierarchical numbering (e.g., 2.2 = story under feature 2, 2.2.1 = subtask). Glen sends audio/video clips between meetings for coordinator to log and queue for Monday review.

Weekly Monday 1-hour sync between Glen and coordinator: plan, prioritise, and close items.

## Decisions / Insights

- Glen decided: SLT meeting should show RYG by department with top 3 priorities, owner, due date, and dependencies -- not agenda items or free discussion
- Glen observed: the EP or meeting owner should run the SLT meeting, not build or chase it -- building and chasing is a coordinator function
- Glen decided: three distinct meeting tiers prevent information overload and ensure each tier has appropriate scope

Worklist prioritisation session between Glen Pryer (CPO/NBI) and Magnus Pryer (NBI producer embedded at CH). 2026-07-23. CH is approximately 55-70 people, multiple departments, no previously structured leadership meeting cadence. Relevant when: embedding into a mid-size studio (40-80 people) that lacks structured leadership meeting cadence -- the three-tier model provides immediate structure without over-engineering.
