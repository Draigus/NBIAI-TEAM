# Design Org Junior-Heavy Risk: Lead Gameplay Designer as Priority Senior Hire

## Key Content

Diagnosis and headcount correction at a ~60-person MMO studio with a junior-heavy design team:

**Symptoms of a junior-heavy design org:** With two seniors (Game Director + one senior designer) and the rest of the team very junior, there are no T-shaped employees. The juniors are "small plus-shaped at best" -- narrow specialist capability, not broad contextual judgment. Day-to-day design decisions lack experienced peer challenge.

**Missing role: Lead Gameplay Designer.** The gap is specifically a lead who covers moment-to-moment feel, 3Cs (character, camera, controls), and combat systems. Without this role, combat and systems design defaults to the Game Director, which creates a bottleneck and leaves moment-to-moment feel underexamined.

**Decision: swap a systems designer headcount for a senior lead.** One systems designer position was de-prioritised to fund a more senior Lead Gameplay Designer hire. That one person should cover systems, combat, and moment-to-moment -- consolidating three specialist areas under one experienced lead.

**Audio independence:** Audio was flagged for removal from the design department to become an independent function, reducing the design director's span of control.

**Sequencing caution:** Unbudgeted (purple) headcount roles across all teams need sequencing together, not by department in isolation.

## Decisions / Insights

- Studio CPO decided: swap one systems designer for a Lead Gameplay Designer covering 3Cs, combat, and systems -- the senior leverage is higher value than an additional junior specialist
- Studio CPO concluded: no T-shaped employees in a junior-heavy design team creates fragility; every decision escalates to the Game Director
- Studio CPO flagged: audio should be independent of design at a studio of this type and size

## Context

Headcount planning session between CPO and Game Director at a ~60-person UK-based MMO studio, July 2026. Studio in vertical slice phase, ~6-10 weeks from VS completion, targeting early production entry post-VS.

## Applicability

Relevant when: auditing a design team at a ~50-80 person studio -- junior-heavy design orgs without T-shaped employees create bottlenecks at the Game Director level; the first senior design hire should be a lead with broad system coverage, not another specialist.
Relevant when: headcount planning under budget constraint -- swapping a junior or mid-level specialist for a senior lead frequently delivers more leverage per hire.
Relevant when: a studio reports that their game "lacks feel" or combat is inconsistent -- a missing Lead Gameplay Designer (covering 3Cs) is often the structural cause.
Relevant when: advising on org chart for an MMO or complex online game -- audio independence from design reduces design director span of control at the point where production complexity peaks.
