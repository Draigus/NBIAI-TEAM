# CH New Player Experience: Single-Player Instancing and Balloon World Reveal

## Key Content

Couch Heroes locked the new player experience (NPE) design for their MMO. The first 30-60 minutes of the game are single-player only.

**Tutorial Cave (locked):**
- Isolated single-player instance; no other players visible
- Emotional intent: isolation and mystery
- Solves the MMO launch population problem ("200 players killing the same boar")

**Portal Peak (locked):**
- Cave exit leading to a hilltop; also single-player, phased
- Cloud cover provides natural instance boundary
- Players never revisit (physically high in the clouds; one-time experience)
- Duration: approximately 30-60 minutes total for Cave + Portal Peak

**World entry mechanic:**
- After Portal Peak: players balloon down into the open multiplayer valley
- Green faction (Drifters Cross) provides the in-world narrative logic for the balloon
- First balloon ride introduces the mechanic; second balloon ride is a reward on Portal Peak completion

**World reveal:**
- Second balloon flight takes players over Downtime city before landing
- Introduces the city visually before the player sets foot in it
- Gary's flyover concept incorporated into the locked design

**Direction status:**
- All four senior stakeholders aligned: Glen, Vardis, David, Robin
- Robin to communicate to Gary; Gary to proceed on this basis

## Decisions / Insights

- Glen, Vardis, David, Robin decided: NPE is single-player from Tutorial Cave through Portal Peak; no random players in this section.
- Glen decided: cloud cover as instance boundary for Portal Peak; players never return to this area.
- Leadership decided: two balloon rides (mechanic introduction + completion reward); second ride provides the Downtime city flyover reveal.
- Glen decided: Drifters Cross faction owns the balloon mechanic with in-world narrative logic.

## Context

Alignment session at Couch Heroes, 7 Jul 2026. Tutorial Cave and Portal Peak direction was contested; this session locked it with four-stakeholder sign-off. The "200 players killing one boar" problem was cited as the explicit rationale for single-player instancing at launch.

## Applicability

- Relevant when: reviewing CH game direction -- Cave + Portal Peak are single-player locked; any design that assumes shared instances in this section is out of scope.
- Relevant when: advising on MMO new player experience design -- single-player instancing for the first 30-60 minutes is the solution to the population density problem at launch.
- Relevant when: CH is designing world reveal moments -- the balloon flyover over Downtime is the locked reveal mechanic; it precedes landing in the city.
- Relevant when: the Drifters Cross faction is being designed -- they own the balloon mechanic narratively; this is a fixed design constraint.
