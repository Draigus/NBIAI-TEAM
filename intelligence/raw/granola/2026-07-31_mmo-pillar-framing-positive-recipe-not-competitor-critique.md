# MMO Positioning: Distinctive Recipe, Not Competitor Critique

## Key Content

Framing a new MMO by contrasting it with established games' failures is the wrong approach. The pattern to avoid and the pattern to use:

**Avoid:** "Rather than X, we do Y." This frames the pitch as a critique of games the audience loves, inviting defensive pushback. It also alienates stakeholders (including founders) who have thousands of hours in WoW, Final Fantasy, or RuneScape.

**Use:** "Those are great games, for different reasons. Here's why we think we can be great." Position on distinctive strengths. No value judgement on competitors.

**Battlefield/Call of Duty analogy:** bullet drop is a distinctive feature of Battlefield -- it's never framed as "unlike CoD, which is wrong." It's just what Battlefield is.

**Symptom check:** the phrase "rather than..." anywhere in a deck is a signal the author is in critique mode. Cut every instance.

**Alternative differentiation device (Simon's chart concept):** show competitor focus areas as a matrix (WoW focuses on X, Final Fantasy on Y, RuneScape on Z) then show where this game sits -- achieves differentiation without confrontation.

**Trade-offs are worth owning honestly:** classless/levelless removes the treadmill but makes balancing harder. Community-affecting events mean not everyone experiences everything. Honest trade-offs build more trust than defensive framing.

## Decisions / Insights

- CPO decided: cut all "rather than..." phrasing from the pillars deck -- reframe as distinctive recipe
- CPO concluded: any critique of established titles alienates audience members who love those titles, including the founding team
- CPO proposed: competitor matrix chart showing where each game focuses vs. where this game focuses as a confrontation-free differentiation method
