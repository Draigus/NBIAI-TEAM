# VC Round Readiness Deliverables for Early-Stage Game Studios

## Key Content

For a ~$10M VC round on an in-development game, investors expect a mature, fully packaged set of materials -- not a prototype deck. A studio 3-4 years into development with ~2 years to launch cannot go to market with informal materials regardless of founder confidence.

Required deliverables (minimum bar):
1. Cash flow projection (month-by-month, 18-24 months)
2. Headcount plan (by role, department, start date, cost)
3. Revenue forecast with assumptions stated
4. Market reach / addressable market sizing
5. Playable demo (for games, this replaces traditional product demo)
6. Full financial model (LTV, CAC, retention assumptions)

September milestone framing: investors at this stage want to see the ideation-to-architecture-to-execution pipeline demonstrated -- not just the idea or the vision. The transition from chaos to structured execution is the signal they're buying.

Founder experience matters: a founder who has never raised a $10M VC round should explicitly defer to those who have on what materials are required, rather than assuming informal materials suffice.

## Decisions / Insights

- Glen concluded: formal materials are non-negotiable for a $10M VC round; founder pushback on this reflects inexperience with institutional fundraising at this scale
- Glen identified: investors see a 3-4 year development history as a signal they need mature planning materials, not an excuse to skip them
- Pattern identified: "September milestone" framing (demonstrate pipeline execution, not just ideation) is the investor signal test at this development stage
