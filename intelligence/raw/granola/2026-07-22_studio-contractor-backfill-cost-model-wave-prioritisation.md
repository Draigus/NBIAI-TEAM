# Studio Contractor Backfill Cost Model and Wave Prioritisation

## Key Content

Standard backfill cost model for contractor exits: one month notice payout + one month dual-contractor overlap = two months fully loaded cost per role. Some roles have no overlap (immediate exit); each backfill requires an explicit decision on overlap or not, plus incremental day-rate delta (replacements often at higher rates than departing juniors).

Per-backfill checklist (required before actioning):
1. Overlap or no overlap? (justify if skipping standard 2-4 week overlap)
2. Incremental cost increase? (day-rate delta between departing and incoming)

Wave prioritisation framework for restructuring / replacement programmes:
- Wave 1: critical roles only, non-negotiable, tied to delivery milestone (e.g. vertical slice closure)
- Wave 2: dialog -- needs to happen, timing is negotiable
- Wave 3: fiscally conditional -- only if revenue or funding milestone is met

Recruiting vendor cost comparison: fixed monthly retainer (~$15K/month) vs contingency model (~15% of salary). At 8 roles averaging $60K, contingency = ~$72K total vs retainer at ~$105K over the same period. Retainer is only cost-effective if hiring volume is consistently high; below threshold, contingency is cheaper.

## Decisions / Insights

- Glen decided: all backfills require an explicit overlap/no-overlap decision and a cost delta assessment before proceeding -- no defaults
- Glen identified: outgoing contractors are often more junior than replacements; ROI of replacement offsets incremental day-rate increase, but must be modelled per role, not assumed
- Pattern identified: wave framing (critical / dialog / conditional) is an effective prioritisation tool for large concurrent replacement programmes
- Pattern identified: recruiting retainer vs contingency breakeven depends on monthly hiring volume -- retainer wins only at sustained high volume

## Context

Operations sync at a 55-70 person studio undertaking a significant contractor replacement programme concurrent with a vertical slice delivery milestone. Attendees: COO, Head of People, Finance lead, CPO (Glen). Hiring plan alignment and backfill modelling session. 2026-07-22.
