# CH Tooling Migration: Confluence + Jira Replacing ClickUp (Jul-Sep 2026)

**Phase 1 -- July 2026:**
- Use existing 10 Confluence seats to populate documentation (design-led)
- Confluence billing: Glen to action company billing starting July
- ClickUp remains live during this phase

**Phase 2 -- August 2026 (start):**
- Roll Confluence out to the whole company
- Begin integrating Jira into Confluence
- Start winding down ClickUp
- AI tools: Claude and Rovo (Confluence's AI) flagged to assist migration

**Phase 3 -- September 2026 (start):**
- Jira open to the whole company for full tracking

**Migration approach:**
- Selective ClickUp-to-Jira import preferred; ClickUp import manager exists but a full dump is not wanted
- Design request forms reverting from Confluence forms back to duplicated Confluence pages (forms not working well)
- Johanna (incoming producer) to load vertical slice workload into Jira

- Glen and EP decided: ClickUp-to-Jira migration is selective, not a full dump; Johanna to handle the import once onboarded.
- Glen and EP decided: design request forms revert to duplicated Confluence pages; the forms feature is not working adequately.
