# Three-Tier Studio Meeting Structure for a Growing Games Studio

## Key Content

A ~55-person MMO studio restructured its meeting cadence to eliminate bleed between strategic, operational, and project-level decisions. The agreed hierarchy:

**Tier 1 -- C-level (leadership only):**
- Strategic decisions, sensitive HR and financial topics
- Decisions made here before being handed to operations for execution
- Small fixed group; no ad-hoc additions

**Tier 2 -- Operations meeting (department leads):**
- Format: each department presents top 5 items, RAG status, new/resolved issues, and asks
- First half: structured updates; second half: overflow or sensitive topics (closed door)
- Code word system: a designated word (e.g. "staffing") signals a red HR issue requiring C-level to stay on; all others can drop
- Agenda shared 1 day in advance; attendees confirmed or adjusted based on topics
- Stakeholders with no relevant agenda items can leave early
- Sensitive decisions (terminations, performance actions) decided at C-level first, then handed to operations for execution only

**Tier 3 -- Project meetings (spin up as needed):**
- Owned by an assigned lead; only relevant people attend
- Progress reported back to operations as a RAG item
- Escalated to C-level only if blocked or requires strategic input

**HR meeting retirement:**
- Dedicated HR meeting retired once operations meeting is running effectively; HR topics absorbed into operations tier with code-word escalation

## Decisions / Insights

- Leadership decided: sensitive decisions (terminations, performance actions) are made at C-level first; operations meeting is the execution layer, not the decision layer.
- Leadership decided: code-word system (e.g. "staffing") signals HR escalation without announcing the topic; C-level stays, others leave.
- Leadership observed: blending strategic, operational, and project-level discussion in a single meeting was causing confusion about decision authority and who needed to be present.
- Leadership decided: agenda shared 1 day ahead; attendance adjusted by topic, not fixed roster.

## Context

Planning meeting at a ~55-person MMO studio during a period of significant leadership restructuring, 7 Jul 2026. The studio had been running a single large meeting covering all tiers; the restructure was triggered by sensitive HR decisions requiring C-level-only handling alongside the regular production cadence.

## Applicability

- Relevant when: a studio's leadership meetings are bloated with mixed audiences -- the three-tier split (C-level, operations, project) is the restructure pattern.
- Relevant when: HR or termination decisions are leaking into production meetings -- designate a C-level-first decision flow with operations as execution layer only.
- Relevant when: a studio needs a code-word system for sensitive HR items -- integrate it into the operations meeting format rather than creating a separate HR meeting.
- Relevant when: advising on meeting cadence for a studio at the 50-100 person scale -- top-5 per department + RAG status is a workable operations meeting format at this headcount.
- Relevant when: a studio's HR meeting is creating duplication -- retire it once operations meeting is running with proper escalation protocols built in.
