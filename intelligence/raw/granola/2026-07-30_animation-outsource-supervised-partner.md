# Animation Outsource: Pre-Existing Relationship + Close Supervision Over Cheapest Vendor

## Key Content

Animation outsource decision at a ~60-person MMO studio in vertical slice phase:

**Selection criteria applied:** Three factors favoured the chosen vendor: (1) cheapest option, (2) pre-existing relationship with the studio, (3) animation-focused studio (not a generalist outsource house). All three combined made this the clear choice over alternatives.

**Key learning from prior failure:** A previous unsupervised outsource engagement (approximately 6 months prior) failed. The diagnosis: no close supervision. The fix for the current engagement is designated internal supervision: the art lead managing the relationship closely, not at arm's length.

**Animation plugin baseline:** Pre-built animations from Unreal plugins (ACF, Traversal) provide approximately 45 core animations covering VS1 and VS2 needs. These are licensed and recognisable (not launchable), but sufficient for investment demos and the vertical slice. The outsource is focused on specific showcase animations (mantling, key combat moves) that are visually distinct and demo-critical.

**Internal animation problem:** Current internal team output is significantly below target throughput. The outsource is a supplement, not a replacement for the internal team rebuild.

## Decisions / Insights

- Studio CPO decided: animation outsource vendor selected on cost + pre-existing relationship + specialisation; not open tendered
- Studio CPO concluded: prior unsupervised outsource failure was a management failure, not a vendor failure -- close supervision by an experienced internal lead is required regardless of vendor quality
- Studio CPO decided: Unreal animation plugins (ACF, Traversal) provide the VS baseline; outsource targets only showcase-quality showcase animations

## Context

Operations meeting at a ~60-person UK/Cyprus-based MMO studio, July 2026. Animation was identified as a red-risk area with internal throughput at roughly 14 animations per 6 months against a target of approximately 3.5x that. Outsource decision made as a bridge to internal team remediation.

## Applicability

Relevant when: a studio is selecting an animation outsource vendor under budget pressure -- cheapest-with-relationship outperforms cheapest-unknown for studios with limited internal supervision bandwidth.
Relevant when: a prior outsource engagement failed -- diagnosis should start with supervision structure, not vendor capability; unguided outsource is the most common failure mode.
Relevant when: a studio needs VS-level animation coverage fast -- Unreal animation plugins (ACF, Traversal) provide a licensed baseline for approximately 45 core animations; outsource budget can be focused on showcase-quality moments only.
Relevant when: internal animation team throughput is below target -- outsource bridges the VS gap; the internal team rebuild runs in parallel and is not deferred.
