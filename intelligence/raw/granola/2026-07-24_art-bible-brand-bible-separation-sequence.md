# Art Bible and Brand Bible: Separation, Sequencing, and Quality Baseline

## Key Content

Common failure mode: a studio conflates its Art Bible (visual production system) with its Brand Bible (marketing and identity layer), or attempts to build a Brand Bible before the Art Bible exists.

**Correct sequencing:**
1. Game pillars first -- define the creative intent
2. Art Bible second -- built from pillars; covers visual production systems
3. Brand Bible third -- a child of the Art Bible; cannot be built from scratch until the Art Bible is solid

**What a complete Art Bible must contain (quality baseline):**
- Colour schemes across zones, factions, states (good/evil, corruption, etc.)
- Good/evil visual language with concrete reference examples
- Depth across aesthetic tones (e.g., grit, myth, metcore) with example breakdowns
- Technical specifications: texture sizes, atlas layouts, model organisation and naming conventions
- Lighting and shading standards
- Visual style delineation: what to avoid, what defines the studio's specific aesthetic

**What a Brand Bible adds on top:**
- Core themes and emotional pillars for marketing
- Primary asset library for promotional use
- Studio style definition distinct from game visual style
- Note: studio branding (company colours, logo system) can differ from in-game branding; both need basic alignment in the Brand Bible

**Common shortcut that fails:** a rough PowerPoint with a few reference images is not an Art Bible. If an Art Director cannot make a decision from it without asking, it is incomplete.

## Decisions / Insights

- Studio leadership decided: Art Bible and Brand Bible are separate documents with a defined dependency: Brand Bible cannot start until Art Bible and pillars are done
- Studio leadership decided: Art Bible templates can be started immediately using existing pillar fragments; Brand Bible blocked until pillars are complete
- Studio leadership observed: a Brand Bible built before the Art Bible is cosmetic -- it will be rebuilt anyway once the Art Bible exists
