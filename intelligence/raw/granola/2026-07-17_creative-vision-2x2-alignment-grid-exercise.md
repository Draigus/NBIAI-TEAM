# Creative Vision 2x2 Alignment Grid: Mapping Studio Leadership on Design Axes

## Key Content

Exercise to surface creative misalignment between studio leadership: map each key creative stakeholder (Game Director, Creative Director, Head of Design, etc.) on a 2x2 grid using two game-design axes relevant to the project.

**Couch Heroes axis set (MMO RPG):**
- X-axis: Choice/impact (high consequence, meaningful decisions) vs. Cozy (low stakes, comfort-forward)
- Y-axis: Solo play vs. Group play

Each stakeholder plots their own position. Discuss the map together to surface where alignment exists and where genuine creative conflict is hidden.

**What the exercise reveals:** stakeholders who say they agree on game direction often hold materially different assumptions about tone, player agency, and social design. The grid makes this visible without requiring a verbal declaration of disagreement.

**Follow-on tool:** the coloured-dot exercise (each person marks their design boundaries with colours: what they will advocate for, what they will accept, what they will resist) extends the 2x2 into a boundary-mapping exercise.

## Decisions / Insights

- Glen (NBI MD / CPO) facilitated: misalignment discovered between two senior creative roles; one position shifted after discussion but the gap remained significant.
- Pattern: creative vision alignment sessions reveal that strong opinions about feel/tone are often held much more firmly than stakeholders initially state -- the first grid position may be "diplomatic"; the second (after discussion) shows the real position.
- Observation: world-state persistence (e.g. burn a house and it stays burned for all players) is a design decision with major compute/cost implications at MMO scale; the 2x2 surfaces this as a real technical constraint, not just a design preference.

## Applicability

Relevant when: onboarding as advisor to a studio and suspecting creative misalignment at leadership level -- run the 2x2 early; it gives everyone a shared visual to discuss without requiring anyone to explicitly call out disagreement.
Relevant when: building a game direction workshop -- the 2x2 is a practical opener that produces a tangible artefact (the map) and surfaces real tension without confrontation.
Relevant when: two senior creative leaders are in nominal agreement but producing conflicting design artefacts -- the 2x2 will show where the real disagreement sits.
Relevant when: advising on live service or MMO game direction -- the solo/group axis and the cozy/challenging axis are the two most commonly contested dimensions; starting there covers the most common misalignment failure modes.
