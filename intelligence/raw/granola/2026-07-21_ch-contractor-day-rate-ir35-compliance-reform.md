# CH Contractor IR35 Compliance Reform: Day Rate Model and Rollout Plan

## Key Content

Couch Heroes was paying contractors for time off (vacation, bereavement leave) -- non-compliant with UK IR35 frameworks. Reform decided: shift from fixed monthly invoices to daily rate billing based on actual days worked.

**Day rate model mechanics:**
- Annual basis: 260 working days minus 36 vacation days minus 8 sick days = 216 billable days
- Monthly soft cap: 20 days maximum; 18 expected average
- Days beyond 20 require manager pre-approval before work, not at invoice stage
- Minimum threshold: 44 days per quarter (80% of 18/month); falling below gives company right to immediate termination (framed as breach of contract, not dismissal)
- Bank holidays and vacation concepts removed entirely for contractors

**Invoicing:** Contractors bill in arrears for days actually worked; payment 7-14 days after invoice; lead manager approval required before payment processed.

**Monitoring:** HiBob overtime approval flow proposed for pre-approving extra days and giving ops real-time visibility. Activity tracking (Slack/Jira/Perforce) agreed as audit index only -- used only on prior suspicion, not as primary monitoring; must never be communicated to staff as surveillance.

**Transition (mid-year challenge):** Effective end of August. Contractors who have taken excess vacation vs. pro-rata will invoice less on their final old-format payment; those who took less receive a top-up. Lorenza to run vacation accrual report first.

**Rollout package per contractor:** Written explanation of changes and why; individual note on vacation position and any adjustment; new contract to sign; open Q&A roundtable.
