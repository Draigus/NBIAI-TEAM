# Virtual Data Room Structure for Game Studio Fundraising

## Key Content

VDR preparation for a game studio Series A/B raise. Work kicks off 1st July with a target of being investor-ready through August.

**Core document categories required in the VDR:**
1. Pitch deck and due diligence deck (pitch deck is investor-facing; DD deck is separate, more detailed)
2. Corporate governance (cap table, shareholder agreements, board resolutions)
3. Financials (P&L, cash flow, projections -- requires CFO/finance lead; takes ~3 weeks to prepare)
4. Tax documentation
5. Commercial contracts (key partnership, publishing, platform agreements)
6. Insurance
7. PII compliance memo (not full GDPR infrastructure -- a one-pager stating intent to comply with OSA/GDPR, timeline, and responsible party is sufficient for a game studio pre-launch)

**Workflow design:**
- Legal work lives in a separate legal breakout space; outputs are replicated into the VDR
- Every item in the VDR needs either a completed document or a one-pager explicitly stating it does not exist yet and why -- gaps that are silent are worse than documented gaps
- Items are sequenced by priority, not work order -- the two are different; confirm priority order with the GC before execution begins

**DD deck dependency:** blocked on the P&L. Finance lead start date is the critical path; allow ~3 weeks from their start to a usable DD-ready P&L.

**Template available:** a live-service MMO P&L template exists to align the incoming finance lead quickly -- use it as the structural starting point.

## Decisions / Insights

- Studio advisor decided: VDR kickoff is 1st July; GC and Head of HR to begin pre-work ahead of that date.
- Studio advisor concluded: PII memo is sufficient pre-launch; full GDPR infrastructure is not required at the Series A/B stage and should not block the data room timeline.
- Studio GC concluded: the existing numbered VDR item list reflects work order only, not investor priority -- needs to be resequenced before the 1st.

## Context

1:1 between studio advisor (Glen) and General Counsel at a ~65-person live-service MMO studio preparing a Series A/B fundraise. VDR target completion: end of August. Named individuals and specific studio anonymised.

## Applicability

- Relevant when: advising a game studio entering a fundraising round -- use this category list as the VDR checklist starting point.
- Relevant when: a studio has not yet completed a raise and is setting up their first VDR -- document all gaps explicitly rather than leaving sections empty.
- Relevant when: the finance function is new or recently hired -- allow 3 weeks from start date before expecting a DD-ready P&L.
- Relevant when: a client asks about GDPR/data compliance requirements for fundraising -- a PII intent memo is sufficient at the pre-launch stage.
- Relevant when: GC and finance are working in parallel on different parts of the DD -- design a legal breakout workflow so the VDR receives polished outputs, not drafts.
