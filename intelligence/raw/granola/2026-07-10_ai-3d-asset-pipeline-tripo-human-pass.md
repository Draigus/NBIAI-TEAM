# AI 3D Asset Pipeline: 90% AI + Human Refinement Pass and AI Specialist Role Definition

## Key Content

A studio CPO validated an AI-first asset creation workflow and defined the structure of an AI specialist hire.

**Asset pipeline model:**
- AI generates assets to approximately 90% completion; artists refine and polish
- Every asset still requires a human pass: ideation, colour direction, prompt ownership, final polish
- PBR (physically based rendering) textures achievable in ~3 hours vs. days by hand, straight to a playable state
- Demonstrated live: Tripo (3D AI generation tool) used to produce a full Unreal Engine-ready 3D model approximately 70 minutes after a concept meeting
- Artist advocacy pattern observed: environment artists who champion AI for others' workflows resist using it for their own -- requires explicit direction and supervision

**AI specialist role definition:**
- One hire cannot cover both code and art AI tooling -- they are distinct skill sets
- Code AI specialist: pipeline automation, tool integration, engine-level generation workflows
- Art AI specialist: prompt ownership, style consistency, AI output QA, human refinement supervision
- Hiring priority: depends on current studio bottleneck; art-first vs. tech-first is a genuine debate

**Energy/environment concern handling:**
- Team raised AI energy concerns; resolved with: AI is already embedded in all productivity tools (Word, Excel, video conferencing); opting out is not meaningful; better to use it effectively

## Decisions / Insights

- Studio CPO decided: AI asset pipeline is standard practice; 90% AI + human refinement pass is the studio's operating model.
- Studio CPO decided: AI specialist role must be split between code and art disciplines.
- Studio CPO observed: an environment artist who championed AI adoption for others resisted it for his own work when confronted directly -- peer advocacy without personal adoption is a compliance risk.
- Studio CPO observed: Tripo can deliver a full Unreal-ready 3D model in under 90 minutes from concept; this resets the timeline benchmark for environment asset creation.

## Context

Leadership sync between studio CPO and Executive Producer at a ~55-person MMO studio, July 2026. Tripo live demonstration occurred in a separate meeting with the game director. Studio adopting AI tooling during active vertical slice production.

## Applicability

- Relevant when: a studio is debating whether to formalise AI in the art pipeline -- use the 90%+human-pass model as a starting operating rule, not a pilot.
- Relevant when: artists express resistance to personal AI adoption while endorsing it for others -- direct confrontation and clear expectation-setting are required.
- Relevant when: a studio is defining an AI specialist hire -- be explicit that code and art AI are different skill sets requiring separate hires.
- Relevant when: a studio's environment art pipeline is a production bottleneck -- Tripo-class tools can materially compress asset delivery time.
- Relevant when: NBI is advising on AI adoption strategy for a game studio -- use this two-hire model as the implementation recommendation.
