# Apple App Store: Social Media Declaration Requirement from July 2026 -- 13+ Minimum Rating for Games with Social Features

**Date:** 2026-07-02
**Domain:** industry_current
**Category:** Platform Policy
**Relevance:** 7 | **Novelty:** 6 | **Actionability:** 7

## Summary

From July 2026, Apple's App Store age rating questionnaire requires developers to declare whether their app includes social media capabilities -- defined as any in-app interaction with user-generated content via a social feed. Apps that declare social media features automatically receive a minimum 13+ rating. The declaration becomes mandatory for all new submissions and updates from September 2026. Separately, Apple is updating its Screen Time parental controls to allow per-category daily limits (Social Media, Entertainment, Games, or Other), changing the parental gating environment for games. Both changes stem from announcements at WWDC June 2026.

## Key Facts

- From July 2026: developers can optionally declare social media capabilities in the App Store age rating questionnaire
- From September 2026: declaration becomes mandatory for all new app submissions and updates, including to alternative marketplaces notarised by Apple
- Automatic effect: any app declaring social media features receives a minimum 13+ age rating, regardless of content otherwise
- Definition of "social media capability": interaction with user-generated content via a social feed within the app
- Screen Time parental controls: updating to per-category daily limits -- Games is one of the four categories; parents can set a blanket Games time allowance rather than managing individual apps
- Apple Games app: editorial team now accepting game developer pitches for promotional In-App Events (new badge types: In-Game Offer, Now On Sale, Try Before You Buy)
- Apple also tightening quality standards: apps in well-established saturated categories (flashlight, fortune telling, simple timers) face removal if not meaningfully differentiated or updated
- Background: changes announced WWDC June 9, 2026

## Strategic Significance

1. **Any game with in-game chat, guilds, UGC, or community feeds now carries an automatic 13+ floor.** This is not a content rating based on violence or themes -- it is a functional classification. An otherwise child-safe puzzle game that includes clan chat or player-posted content will be rated 13+, affecting its discoverability to under-13 audiences and parental control filters.

2. **Per-category Screen Time limits change discoverability and session dynamics.** When parents set a 2-hour Games category limit, all games share the same time budget. Games with high daily engagement (casual loops, daily quests) are competing not just against other games in the store but against their shared time ceiling. The category-level cap is a headwind for session-length-dependent monetisation.

3. **The editorial pitch pathway to the Apple Games app is a new discovery channel.** For studios with appropriate In-App Events, direct pitches to the App Store editorial team can drive significant organic installs. This is an underused lever -- most studios rely on passive featuring rather than active pitching.
