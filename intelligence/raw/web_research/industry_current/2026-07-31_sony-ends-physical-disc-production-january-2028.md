# Sony PlayStation to End Physical Disc Production for New Games in January 2028

**Date:** 2026-07-31
**Domain:** industry_current
**Category:** Platform Policy
**Relevance:** 8 | **Novelty:** 7 | **Actionability:** 7

## Summary

Sony Interactive Entertainment announced on July 1, 2026 via the PlayStation Blog that it will cease physical disc production for new PlayStation games in January 2028. Games released before that date will remain available on disc; all new titles after January 2028 will be digital-only on PlayStation platforms. Sony cited the current 85% digital / 15% physical split across PS4 and PS5 software sales as the basis for the decision. This extract is a late capture: the announcement was in scope for the July 10 research cycle but was not captured at that time.

## Key Facts

- **Announcement date:** July 1, 2026 (PlayStation Blog)
- **Implementation date:** January 2028 -- new game disc production ends
- **Scope:** New releases only; existing physical titles remain available
- **Current digital/physical split (PS4 + PS5):** 85% digital / 15% physical full-game software sales
- **Sony's stated rationale:** "A natural direction for Sony Interactive Entertainment to adapt to consumer trends as the general preference for digital media significantly outpaces physical discs"
- **Physical disc players:** PS5 disc editions and PS4 consoles will continue to play existing disc libraries; the change affects production of new titles only
- **Sony Group CFO Lin Tao** confirmed Sony does not plan to manufacture new disc hardware going forward, responding to media questions on July 30, 2026

## Strategic Significance

1. **The disc retail channel closes for new PlayStation titles in 18 months** January 2028 is 18 months away. Publishers with multi-year production plans who factor physical disc retail (Game, GAME UK, GameStop US, Amazon physical, Argos) into their launch revenue projections need to reforecast. Physical release cost savings are significant; loss of shelf visibility and gifting sales is not. The trade-off is now a forced decision, not a strategic choice.

2. **The 85/15 split makes the timing commercially rational but not risk-free** At 15% physical, production runs, certification costs, and retail distribution logistics are increasingly marginal. However, the 15% is not uniformly distributed: premium packaged collectors' editions, PEGI-age-rated titles requiring physical for retail (particularly in some Eastern European and MENA markets), and chart-positioning through physical point-of-sale are all affected disproportionately. Mid-market studios will feel this more than AAA publishers.

3. **Sets a precedent that accelerates platform-wide consolidation to digital** Sony's move follows Xbox's all-digital Series S and its announced digital-only disc manufacturing exit (separate announcement, 2025). With two of three platform holders digital-only, Nintendo's hybrid physical/digital approach on Switch 2 becomes the last physical retail channel of scale. Physical retail leverage in publishing deals diminishes accordingly.

## NBI Advisory Relevance

- Any NBI client modelling physical release revenue for 2027+ PlayStation launches must update projections: physical disc is unavailable from January 2028 for new titles
- Relevant to Couch Heroes game planning horizon: if The Misfits' MMO targets a 2027-2028 launch on console, the physical distribution channel is not available for PS at launch

- The 85/15 split validates the D2C shift data from the Appcharge/GDC report (2026-07-24 extract): digital dominance is a structural fact, not a trend
