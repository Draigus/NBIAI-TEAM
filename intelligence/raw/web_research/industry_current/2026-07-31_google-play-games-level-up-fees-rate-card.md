# Google Play Games Level Up: 15% Fee Rate and Billing Details Now Confirmed

**Date:** 2026-07-31
**Domain:** industry_current
**Category:** Platform Policy
**Relevance:** 7 | **Novelty:** 5 | **Actionability:** 8

## Summary

Google Play's expanded billing and fee restructure, effective from the July 22, 2026 live date, includes a Games Level Up programme that reduces the standard 30% service fee to 20% automatically for all new installs, with a further reduction to 15% available for studios that meet a defined set of technical and UX requirements. A 5% billing fee applies on top in the US, UK, and European Economic Area. The rate card becomes available September 30, 2026 in US/UK/EEA/Australia, and Japan/South Korea by December 31, 2026. This extract supplements the July 15 extract (2026-07-15_google-play-catalog-interoperability-22-july-live.md), which noted the fee reduction as a concurrent development but did not detail the Level Up requirements.

## Key Facts

- **Baseline fee reduction:** 30% to 20% on one-time purchases for new installs -- automatic, no developer action required (effective July 22)
- **Games Level Up programme:** Reduces fee to 15% on new installs for studios that meet the full requirement set
- **Billing fee (US, UK, EEA):** 5% on top of the service fee, regardless of Level Up participation
- **Effective net rates (US/UK/EEA):** Standard = 25% (20% + 5%); Level Up = 20% (15% + 5%)
- **Rate card availability:** September 30, 2026 (US, UK, EEA, Australia); December 31, 2026 (Japan, South Korea); September 30, 2027 (rest of world)
- **Japan acceleration:** Originally scheduled for end-2026; moved to September 30, 2026 following JFTC consultation and domestic developer pressure
- **Level Up requirements (three categories):**
  - Consistent user journeys: passkey / approved SSO authentication; Restore Credentials for automatic sign-in on device restoration; Google Engage SDK integration
  - Expansive reach: Adaptive App Tier 2 compliance across phone, tablet, and foldable; separate EXT/TV guidelines
  - Stable gameplay: (specific criteria in Google's published guidelines)
- **Source:** Android Developers Blog -- "Expanded billing choice and lower fees on Google Play" (June 2026)

## Strategic Significance

1. **Effective fee is 20-25% in major markets, not 15-20%** The 5% billing fee is additive and applies uniformly in the most commercially significant markets. Studios and publishers modelling "Google reducing to 15%" need to add 5% -- the real floor is 20% for Level Up participants in the US/UK/EEA, versus Apple's standard 30% (or 27% under DMA). The headline rate is more attractive than the effective rate.

2. **Level Up compliance is a genuine technical lift** Passkey integration, Engage SDK, and Adaptive App Tier 2 across phone/tablet/foldable are non-trivial engineering requirements for studios that have not already built to these standards. Studios with legacy auth systems (username/password, older token implementations) face integration work before they can access the 15% rate. The September 30 rate card date gives approximately 60 days from today.

3. **Japan acceleration signals regulatory pressure** Moving Japan from December 2026 to September 30 ahead of the global schedule, explicitly citing JFTC consultation, indicates Google is proactively managing regulatory risk in a jurisdiction where mobile platform fee scrutiny is highest after the Epic v. Google settlement. The pattern suggests further regional accelerations are likely as regulators engage.
