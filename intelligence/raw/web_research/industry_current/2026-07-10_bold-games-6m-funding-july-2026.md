# Bold Games Raises $6M Led by Arcadia Gaming Partners and Makers Fund, July 2026

**Date:** 2026-07-10
**Domain:** industry_current
**Category:** Funding
**Relevance:** 6 | **Novelty:** 6 | **Actionability:** 5

## Summary

Bold Games, an Istanbul-based mobile game development studio, raised $6 million on 8 July 2026 in a round led by Arcadia Gaming Partners and Makers Fund with participation from e2vc and JIMCO. The studio is developing its first mobile puzzle game and intends to use the capital for team growth. This is a small but confirmed data point on which investors are currently active in mobile gaming at seed and early stage.

## Key Facts

- Raise: $6 million, closed 8 July 2026
- Studio: Bold Games, Istanbul, Turkey
- Investors: Arcadia Gaming Partners (lead), Makers Fund (co-lead), e2vc, JIMCO, angels
- Product: mobile puzzle game, first title
- Use of funds: team growth and game development

## Strategic Significance

Makers Fund and Arcadia Gaming Partners remaining active at the $6M mobile early-stage level indicates investor appetite has not fully dried up despite the broader industry contraction. Istanbul-based studios continue to attract international capital, reflecting the sustained cost advantage and talent pool of Turkish mobile development.
