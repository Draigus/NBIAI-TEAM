# Xbox Cuts 3,200 Jobs and Divests Four Studios in Largest Single Gaming Layoff on Record

**Date:** 2026-07-10
**Domain:** industry_current
**Category:** M&A / Layoffs
**Relevance:** 10 | **Novelty:** 10 | **Actionability:** 9

## Summary

On 6 July 2026, Microsoft announced the elimination of 3,200 Xbox roles (roughly 20% of the gaming division) and the divestiture of four studios: Ninja Theory and Undead Labs entering new ownership with funding to complete Senua's Saga and State of Decay 3; Double Fine and Compulsion Games returning to independence with full IP ownership. A fifth studio, Arkane Lyon (Dishonored, Deathloop), is in French Works Council consultation over potential closure, with Marvel's Blade simultaneously cancelled. This is Xbox's fifth major layoff round since the $68.7 billion Activision Blizzard acquisition closed in October 2023.

## Key Facts

- 3,200 roles eliminated, approximately 20% of Xbox gaming division
- 1,600 jobs cut on 6 July 2026; remainder over the following 12 months
- Ninja Theory (Cambridge, Hellblade) sold to new owner with mandate to complete Senua's next game
- Undead Labs (~110 employees, State of Decay series) sold to new owner
- Double Fine (San Francisco, Psychonauts) returning to independence with IP
- Compulsion Games (Montreal, South of Midnight, We Happy Few) returning to independence with IP
- Arkane Lyon in Works Council consultation; Marvel's Blade AAA title cancelled
- Fifth major Xbox layoff event since October 2023 Activision Blizzard close
- Industry cumulative losses 2022-mid 2025: approximately 45,000 jobs

## Strategic Significance

1. **Platform-holder integration of mid-size studios has failed to produce the expected economies of scale.** Studios with strong IP but uncertain commercial positioning are being returned to independence rather than shut down outright -- a structural shift in how platform holders manage portfolio risk.

2. **The cancellation of Marvel's Blade signals that licensed AAA titles without a clear release window are the first to go when capital is constrained.** Licensed IP reduces creative risk but not execution risk; the pattern suggests platform holders are rationalising licensed projects earlier in the cycle.

3. **The cumulative 45,000 industry job losses since 2022 represent a structural capacity reduction.** Production timelines and team availability will be affected for years. Studios returning to independence with known IP (Double Fine, Compulsion) become acquisition or co-development targets at distressed valuations.

## NBI Advisory Relevance

- Double Fine, Compulsion, Ninja Theory, and Undead Labs are newly independent with known IP -- potential partnership or co-development targets for any client assessing acquisition candidates
- A significant pool of experienced developers is entering the market over the next 12 months; relevant for any client in talent acquisition mode
- The divestiture pattern is worth tracking for which buyers emerge -- names not yet confirmed publicly as of 10 July 2026
- Arkane Lyon's Works Council outcome (closure vs. sale) is a near-term watch item
