# Sony PlayStation Ending Physical Disc Production for New Games from January 2028

**Date:** 2026-07-03
**Domain:** industry_current
**Category:** Platform
**Relevance:** 9 | **Novelty:** 9 | **Actionability:** 8

## Summary

Sony Interactive Entertainment announced on July 1, 2026, that physical disc production for all new PlayStation titles will cease from January 2028. Existing titles released before that date in disc format are unaffected. Sony cited that nearly four in five full-game purchases on PS4/PS5 over the past year were digital. Physical retailers will remain in the distribution chain, transitioning to digital code cards or vouchers rather than being cut from the supply chain entirely.

## Key Facts

- Effective date: January 2028 for new releases only; existing disc titles unaffected
- Approximately 80% of full-game PS4/PS5 purchases in the past 12 months were digital (per Sony)
- Physical retail distribution to continue via digital code cards or voucher format
- PS Store on PS3 closing in Latin America and the Middle East in late 2026; all other regions July 2027
- Nintendo Switch 2 not expected to follow suit per Circana analyst Mat Piscatella
- No impact on pre-existing physical catalogue titles

## Strategic Significance

1. **The largest console platform formalises the transition to digital-only.** This is not a soft signal -- it is a production cutoff with a date. Every studio currently planning a physical release strategy for a 2028+ title needs to reassess. Disc as a distribution format will exist only for back-catalogue.

2. **Physical retail relationship is not severed, it is restructured.** Sony is preserving shelf presence for gaming through code cards. This matters for studios and publishers whose marketing budgets depend on physical retail promotional placement -- those arrangements continue, but the product format changes.

3. **Revenue mix modelling is affected immediately.** Any financial model for a 2028+ PlayStation title that includes a physical SKU revenue stream needs revising now. The physical premium (box, artwork, collector editions) survives via third-party limited-print publishers (Limited Run Games model), not first-party Sony production.

## NBI Advisory Relevance

- Any NBI client working on a 2028+ title with a physical release strategy needs this flagged in production and revenue planning; the disc format is officially off the table for new Sony-published titles
- Publisher selection advisory: EA (post-PIF close), Ubisoft, and other major third parties will be forced to make equivalent announcements; the effective industry timeline for disc death is now set
- Physical retail advisory for any client with distribution deals: digital code cards are a structurally different margin and logistics model; contracts written to disc specs need amendment
- Collector edition revenue strategy can still use physical manufacturing via third-party publishers, but needs to be decoupled from the main release plan
