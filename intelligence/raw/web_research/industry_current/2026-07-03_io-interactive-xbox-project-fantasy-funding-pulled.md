# IO Interactive Loses Xbox Funding for Project Fantasy RPG, Begins Layoffs

**Date:** 2026-07-03
**Domain:** industry_current
**Category:** M&A / Layoffs
**Relevance:** 8 | **Novelty:** 7 | **Actionability:** 7

## Summary

Microsoft Xbox pulled its publishing deal and funding from IO Interactive's online fantasy RPG (Project Fantasy) on or around June 30, 2026, citing a "fresh look at where we invest." IO Interactive -- the Copenhagen-based studio behind Hitman and 007: First Light, which launched to commercial success just weeks prior -- confirmed the partnership ended abruptly and that "staffing decisions" were a direct consequence. The studio said it remains committed to Project Fantasy and is actively seeking a replacement publisher or self-publishing route.

## Key Facts

- Project Fantasy: online fantasy RPG, announced 2023, developed at IO Interactive's Malmö studio (Sweden)
- Xbox confirmed withdrawal, stated it is "focusing on highest priorities"
- IO Interactive released 007: First Light in May/June 2026 to commercial success
- Studio confirmed immediate "staffing decisions" following the funding withdrawal; no headcount disclosed
- IO Interactive is an independent Danish studio (Copenhagen HQ), not a Microsoft first-party
- This withdrawal is separate from the 5,500-person Xbox internal layoffs wave; it affects a third-party co-publishing deal
- IO Interactive is seeking alternative publisher or exploring self-publishing for Project Fantasy

## Strategic Significance

1. **Xbox Reset extends beyond first-party studios into the third-party publishing portfolio.** The cuts to Ninja Theory, Arkane Lyon, and five internal studios have already been confirmed. The IO Interactive withdrawal shows the reset is also terminating external co-publishing agreements -- a different and previously underreported category of exposure.

2. **Commercial success provides no insulation from the reset.** IO Interactive shipped a well-received game (007: First Light) mere weeks before the funding was pulled. The decision was budget-driven at the Xbox level, not performance-driven at the studio level. This directly parallels the Ninja Theory situation (Hellblade 2 was critically acclaimed before the studio was closed).

3. **Self-publishing at scale is a credible fallback for a studio of IO's size.** IO Interactive is independently owned, has a track record of Hitman self-publishing under the episodic model, and has revenue from 007. The question is whether a fantasy MMO-adjacent online RPG can be sustained without platform publishing support. This is a different risk profile than the internal Microsoft studios facing closure.

## NBI Advisory Relevance

- Any client in negotiations with Xbox for a co-publishing or co-development deal should treat this as evidence that the Reset is consuming external deals, not just internal studios; commercial pipeline does not protect third-party agreements
- For NBI clients exploring self-publishing routes: IO Interactive's situation is a live case study in what a credible self-publishing pivot looks like at a mid-sized AAA studio
- For talent advisory: IO Interactive Malmö will be reducing headcount; qualified online/RPG developers entering the market shortly
- The pattern of "funded project cancelled, developer must find new publisher mid-production" is now common enough to constitute a structural advisory risk clients should be modelling in any deal with a major platform holder
