# Microsoft Redirects Obsidian to New Fallout Game, Cancels Multiple Projects, Cuts ~25% of Studio

**Date:** 2026-07-10
**Domain:** industry_current
**Category:** M&A
**Relevance:** 7 | **Novelty:** 8 | **Actionability:** 7

## Summary

Bloomberg reported on 8 July 2026 that Microsoft has cancelled multiple unannounced Obsidian Entertainment projects and is redirecting the studio to a new Fallout game. Approximately 25% of Obsidian's workforce is being laid off as part of the pivot. This is a separate event from the 6 July studio divestitures and represents Microsoft concentrating its remaining first-party resources around proven franchise IP rather than new IP development.

## Key Facts

- Reported by Bloomberg on 8 July 2026
- Obsidian Entertainment: multiple unannounced projects cancelled
- Studio redirected to new Fallout franchise entry
- Approximately 25% of Obsidian workforce laid off
- Separate from the 6 July announcement of 3,200 cuts and four studio divestitures
- Pattern: Xbox consolidating surviving studios around established franchise IP (Fallout, Halo, Forza) and exiting new IP development risk
- Fallout franchise value reinforced by Amazon TV series; commercially proven ceiling

## Strategic Significance

1. **Microsoft is not simply cutting costs but actively pivoting its remaining first-party studios away from new IP development toward known franchises.** This has knock-on effects on external developer pitches to platform holders and publisher appetite for licensing versus original development.

2. **Platform-holder appetite for new IP has contracted significantly.** The Obsidian pivot, combined with the six July divestitures, confirms this is a structural strategic shift rather than a cost cycle.

3. **Licensed IP from entertainment (TV, film) carries a lower risk premium than original gaming IP at present.** The Fallout TV halo effect is a direct factor in the Obsidian redirect decision.

## NBI Advisory Relevance

- Any client in early-stage new IP development pitching to platform holders should factor in that first-party funding for unproven IP has materially contracted
- NBI advisory should redirect clients toward co-development models with proven IP holders, or toward multiplatform self-publishing using the newly opened Android channels
- The Obsidian pivot also signals that licensed entertainment IP (TV, film) as a game basis carries a meaningfully lower risk premium at platform holders than original gaming IP
- Obsidian developers affected by the layoffs are potential talent acquisition targets for NBI clients in RPG or open-world development
