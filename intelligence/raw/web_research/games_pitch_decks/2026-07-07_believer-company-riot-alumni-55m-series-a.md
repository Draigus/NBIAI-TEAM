# The Believer Company -- Ex-Riot VP + Founding Member, $55M Series A on Vision Alone, March 2023

## Key Content

The Believer Company launched publicly on 7 March 2023 with a $55 million Series A secured before announcing any product. Announced at the height of VC market tightening -- the founders themselves acknowledged timing uncertainty: "we also aren't sure what fundraising looks like later this year or next."

**Round:** $55M Series A
**Lead:** Lightspeed Venture Partners
**Co-investors:** Andreessen Horowitz (a16z), BITKRAFT Ventures, Riot Games, 1Up Ventures, Cleveland Avenue (Don Thompson), Tornante Company (Michael D. Eisner)

**Founders:**
- Michael Chow (CEO) -- ex-Riot Games VP; executive producer on League of Legends: Wild Rift; co-founder of Newtoy (Words with Friends developer, acquired by Zynga)
- Steven Snow (CPO) -- founding member of Riot Games; executive-produced League of Legends to its initial commercial peak

**Executive team at launch (fully staffed, no game):**
- Landon McDowell (CTO) -- Microsoft, Riot, Linden Lab
- Jeremy Vanhoozer (CCO) -- Bungie, Electronic Arts
- Tim Hsu (COO) -- Twitter, Riot Games
- Shankar Gupta-Harrison (CMO) -- Riot Games, Dentsu X
- Jeff Jew (VP Design) -- League of Legends, Legends of Runeterra

**Pitch narrative:** Market thesis, not game concept. The pitch argued that the open-world genre has been "capped by a single-player, boxed-product focus" and that the market was ready for multiplayer open-world adventures built from the ground up for co-operative play. The team invoked the Pixar model: not inventing new technology but applying existing tools to create new types of experience.

No game announced. Development timeline stated as 3-5 years.

**Investor quote on record:** Michael Eisner (Tornante): "Truly great content franchises need to resonate across games, film, television, collectibles, and experiences." This signals Tornante's investment rationale was transmedia franchise upside, not just gaming revenue.

Riot Games investing as a co-investor is a documented pitch accelerant -- a public endorsement from the founders' former employer, functioning as a reference that institutional VCs cannot replicate.

## Decisions / Insights

Believer is the cleanest documented case of what tier-1 Riot pedigree specifically unlocks: Lightspeed as lead, a16z participation, and strategic investors with IP/transmedia expertise. The pitch was not a game -- it was a thesis about an underserved structural gap (multiplayer open world) delivered by a team whose track record made the thesis credible without a prototype.

Three elements combined to justify the $55M size on a vision pitch:
1. Founding pedigree at the company-formation level, not just individual contributor credits (VP and co-founder, not senior designer)
2. A fully-staffed executive team across all functions at launch -- signals execution readiness, not just a two-person founding team with a concept
3. Transmedia franchise framing that gave Tornante/Eisner a value creation thesis independent of game sales

The timing is also instructive: Snow cited uncertainty about future fundraising windows. The $55M was raised in the last accessible window before VC contraction bit into gaming (the Believer raise was 7 March 2023; gaming VC deal sizes contracted materially through the rest of 2023 and 2024).

## Context

As of mid-2026 the studio remains in stealth. No game has been announced. The 3-5 year development timeline stated in March 2023 would place a first announcement in the 2026-2028 range. The raise pre-dates the post-2023 VC tightening, making the $55M figure exceptional relative to what the same pitch would attract in 2025.

## Applicability

**For NBI:** This is the benchmark case for ex-Riot (and Blizzard/Bungie-equivalent) C-suite alumni pitching a pre-product studio formation. Directly usable when an NBI client asks: "how much can I raise, and from whom, without a prototype?"

The pitch structure is replicable in principle but not in scale without comparable credentials: a fully-staffed exec team at launch and founding-team-level pedigree (VP/co-founder, not senior developer) are prerequisites for the $55M+ range. Mid-level alumni cannot replicate this -- but the structural lesson (market gap thesis + operational readiness signalling) applies at smaller scales.

Also useful as a reminder that the Believer raise was timed to a closing window. A structurally similar pitch today would more likely land in the $10-20M range given VC tightening.

**Applicable when:** NBI client is C-suite or co-founding-team-level alumni from a tier-1 studio, has a specific market positioning thesis, and is building a multi-function founding team rather than a single-creator studio. a16z and Lightspeed are the documented primary VC targets for this profile.
