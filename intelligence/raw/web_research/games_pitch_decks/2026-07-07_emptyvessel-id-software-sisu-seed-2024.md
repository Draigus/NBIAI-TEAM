# emptyvessel -- id Software / Doom Alumni, Anti-Bloat Formation Pitch, Sisu Game Ventures Seed, March 2024

## Key Content

emptyvessel was announced on 12 March 2024 as an independent AAA studio, operating fully remote across the US, Australia, and Europe.

**Founders:**
- Garrett Young (CEO/GM) -- 25+ years; former Executive Producer at Microsoft and Activision; former Director of Production at Disney; former General Manager at id Software
- Emanuel Palalic (Game Director) -- senior character artist at id Software; shipped Doom (2016) and Doom: Eternal

**Additional leads at launch:** CTO Wei Ning; Art Director Alex Palma; Animation Director Rico Flores. Development team drawn from Call of Duty, The Last of Us, Borderlands, Tomb Raider, Uncharted, and Callisto Protocol.

**Funding:** Undisclosed seed amount, led by Sisu Game Ventures. Co-investors: Bill Munk (co-owner, Tripwire Interactive); Dan Bunting (former co-studio head, Treyarch, Call of Duty franchise). Advisory board includes Riley Russell (former Sony SVP and 33-year veteran, now Kojima Productions EVP).

**Game:** Unannounced immersive shooter set in a dystopian sci-fi world, built in Unreal Engine 5. Composer: Mick Gordon (Doom, Doom: Eternal, Borderlands 3, Prey).

**Pitch narrative -- explicit anti-bloat thesis:**
The founders used the 2024 industry layoff wave (8,000+ jobs cut in the first months of 2024) and AAA cost inflation as the macro backdrop for the formation pitch. Stated framing: "Our industry is at a crossroads between business and talent." Core thesis: AAA-quality output is achievable with smaller, focused teams and "more attainable scope."

Specific reference point cited publicly: Spider-Man 2's $300M budget requiring 7M+ sales just to break even. The pitch was not primarily about the game concept -- it was about a production model that the founders argued is both more sustainable for developers and more viable for investors.

The "Developer-First" framing was also a talent acquisition tool: targeting displaced senior developers from the 2024 layoff wave who wanted to work in a different operating environment.

## Decisions / Insights

emptyvessel represents the most explicitly formulated anti-bloat pitch in the 2024 studio formation wave. What makes it structurally interesting for NBI:

**The pitch combined three elements that work together:**
1. An operational thesis (smaller teams, developer wellbeing, attainable scope) that is specific and falsifiable -- not generic "we love games"
2. A genre positioning (immersive shooter) that anchors the thesis to a market category
3. Industry dysfunction as macro tailwind -- 8,000+ layoffs and a $300M Spider-Man 2 budget are facts that make the "AAA is broken" framing credible without the founders having to argue it themselves

**Sisu as the primary investor:** Sisu Game Ventures' documented investment criteria are "first money in, pre-revenue, pre-product, teams not IPs." emptyvessel fits their thesis exactly -- production pedigree from id/Activision/Microsoft, pre-product, Unreal Engine 5. For NBI clients pitching a similar profile (AAA production background, no prototype, developer-first ethos), Sisu is the documented first-call investor.

**Operator co-investors as production credibility signals:** Bill Munk (Tripwire co-owner, Killing Floor/Chivalry II background) and Dan Bunting (Treyarch, shipped multiple mainline Call of Duty titles) are not generalist VCs -- they are practitioners who can personally evaluate whether id Software veterans can run a studio. Their inclusion alongside Sisu signals to institutional follow-on investors that independent domain experts have already validated the team's production capability.

**Mick Gordon as quality signal without playable content:** Attaching a composer with a recognisable franchise portfolio (Doom, Doom: Eternal, Borderlands 3) is a credible IP quality indicator that requires no prototype. The same principle applies to art directors, narrative directors, and other creative leads from recognisable titles.

## Context

Announced during the height of the 2024 gaming industry layoff wave. The timing was deliberate: the founders positioned the studio as both a solution to a systemic industry problem and a talent magnet for developers who wanted an alternative operating environment. The advisory board (Riley Russell/Sony/Kojima Productions) provides AAA distribution credibility independent of the founding team's id Software credits.
