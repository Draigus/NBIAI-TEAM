# Konvoy Ventures -- Mobile F2P Investment Framework -- Investor Thesis Synthesis 2022-2025

## Key Content

Konvoy Ventures ($258M AUM, Josh Chapman Managing Partner) is a thesis-driven gaming VC investing from seed through Series B. Their newsletter archive contains substantive mobile market analysis that reveals implicit investment criteria even where explicit evaluation rubrics are not published.

**Mobile monetisation structural shift (2022-2023):** Konvoy documented the inverse relationship emerging in mobile: 8% download growth and 8% DAU growth alongside a 5% revenue decline in 2022. The IDFA/AAID deprecation eliminated device-level targeting, compressing ad revenue per impression across the board. Hypercasual models (circa 94% ad-revenue dependent) became structurally impaired. Konvoy's published position: studios requiring advertising monetisation as primary revenue are impaired assets; studios with IAP-driven models and genuine retention are the durable investments.

Mobile retention benchmarks Konvoy cites for hypercasual: D1=25%, D7=7%, D30=2%. These are the category floor. Any mobile studio pitching casual mechanics to Konvoy needs metrics that meaningfully exceed these benchmarks or a structural explanation for why their genre dynamics differ. Revenue per genre in 2022: 7 of the top 8 mobile genres saw IAP value decline while 6 saw download increases. Konvoy's explicit conclusion: download growth is not a fundable thesis; retention depth and monetisation quality are.

**Series A crunch data (Aug 2024):** Konvoy published specific data on gaming studio Seed-to-Series-A graduation rates using Carta's VC fund performance database. Only 2.3% of gaming startups that raised a Seed round in Q1 2022 successfully raised a Series A within 2 years -- compared to 15.4% of all Seed-stage startups across sectors. This represents a gaming-specific failure rate roughly 6x worse than the broader market. 42% of successful Series A gaming companies are content (game) studios; the majority of successful gaming Series A raises are infrastructure, platform, or tools companies. Content studios face a structurally harder path to Series A than platform companies within the same asset class.

**What Konvoy signals about fundable positioning:** Studios that have "right-sized their businesses and focused on profitability" are better positioned for the current capital environment. Companies targeting growth equity ($20-200M checks) need to demonstrate they are within 3-5 years of a credible exit. The dry powder problem: 72% of dry powder from 2020-2022 vintage gaming funds remained uncalled as of 2024, meaning capital exists but deployment criteria have tightened materially.

**Mobile web shop economics (2025):** Konvoy's most recent mobile analysis documents the web shop adoption wave. 72% of top-grossing mobile games operate web shops as of 2025. Studios capture 5-10% processing fees via web shops versus 15-30% platform fees -- approximately a 20% margin improvement translating to a 40-60% profit uplift in mature operations. Scopely, Supercell, and King are early movers; mid-tier studios access the same economics through Xsolla or Appcharge. Investment implication stated explicitly: web shop adoption reshapes unit economics, improves cash flow visibility, and creates direct customer data access -- all factors that enhance valuation multiples.

**Hard exclusions at Konvoy (stated):** Pure gambling or social casino plays, blockchain/web3 without genuine gaming utility, non-technical founding teams.

## Decisions / Insights

- The 2.3% Seed-to-Series-A graduation rate for gaming content studios (Q1 2022 cohort) is the single most important market context figure for NBI pitch advisory. Clients need to understand they are competing in a structurally tightened environment, not the 2021 market.
- Hypercasual retention floor (D1=25%, D7=7%, D30=2%) is the threshold against which any casual mobile pitch is evaluated at Konvoy. Exceeding these is table stakes; meaningfully exceeding them with an explanation of why is the pitch.
- Web shop adoption is now a valuation signal, not a technical footnote. Any studio pitching mobile F2P without a web shop roadmap looks behind the curve to thesis-driven investors in 2025-2026.
- Konvoy's check size ($1-3M at lead) makes them a seed and bridge investor, not a Series A lead for content studios. NBI clients should position Konvoy as a seed syndicate member or early validator, not the primary Series A target.

## Context

Konvoy is active with a weekly newsletter that is one of the better public sources of mobile market data and investor thesis transparency. Their Fund III focus areas include AI game development tools, UGC platforms, and mobile F2P content. As noted above, their typical check size positions them as seed-stage support for content studios; growth equity for content at Series A typically requires Arcadia, a16z Games, Griffin Gaming Partners, or BITKRAFT as lead.
