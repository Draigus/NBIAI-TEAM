# Alumni Pedigree Premium -- Quantitative Analysis of Ex-Riot Studio Raises, 2020-2024

## Key Content

InvestGame's "The Alumni Effect" study analysed studios founded by Riot Games alumni that raised institutional capital between 2020 and 2024.

**Ex-Riot headline data (directly verified):**
- Total raised: $485M across 38 funding rounds
- Studios covered: approximately 27-30 that secured investments
- Average early-stage deal size for ex-Riot studios: $11.2M
- Gaming industry average: $7.3M
- Premium: 53% above industry average (quoted directly in the article)
- a16z: 13 rounds, $339M cumulative value across the ex-Riot cohort -- dominant single investor in this set

**Follow-on funding:** The article states a substantially higher proportion of ex-Riot teams secured follow-on financing within 2-3 years compared to the broader VC gaming market average of 13-15% during the 2021-2022 peak. Specific ex-Riot follow-on percentage not disclosed in article.

**Studio breakdown by focus area:**
- 18 studios: PC and console (6 of these also covering mobile)
- 6 projects: web3/esports
- 1 platform: VENN (shut down)
- 1 backend: Pragma ($38.2M total raised)
- 1 mobile AR: Aglet (shut down)

**Notable funded studios from the cohort:** Theorycraft Games (SUPERVIVE -- $87.5M raised, shut down Feb 2026), Singularity Six/Palia ($30M+ raised, studio closed summer 2024), Believer Company ($55M Series A, in development), Pragma (B2B gaming backend).

**Ex-ATVI companion data (from second InvestGame article, not independently verified in this session):**
- 28-30 studios, 40-45 rounds, $700M+ total
- Average deal size peaked at $10.7M in 2021, compressed to $4.2M by 2023
- Notable outcomes: Second Dinner ($100M Series B after Marvel Snap, 28M downloads, $200M mobile IAP); Firewalk Studios (Sony-backed Concord -- shut down 2 weeks post-launch)

## Decisions / Insights

The 53% deal size premium is the most directly usable number in this dataset. When an NBI client asks "why should I get a higher valuation than an unknown indie team?", this is the answer: ex-Riot alumni command $11.2M where the industry averages $7.3M.

The follow-on rate data matters equally: ex-Riot studios are roughly twice as likely to secure follow-on financing. This means seed VCs can pitch their own LPs on a better return-path narrative when backing a Riot alumni team, which is why the investor behaviour follows.

The failure cases in the cohort are as instructive as the successes. Theorycraft (SUPERVIVE) raised $87.5M on a "10,000-hour competitive game" thesis backed by a16z, Makers Fund, and NEA -- and shut down 5 months after 1.0 launch with 400 concurrent players. Singularity Six/Palia raised $30M+ and closed without achieving commercial viability. Firewalk/Concord raised at Sony-level budgets and lasted 2 weeks live. The pedigree premium accelerates access to capital; it does not protect against product-market fit failures.

Post-2022 shift: deal sizes for ATVI alumni compressed from $10.7M (2021) to $4.2M (2023). This tracks with broader VC tightening and the documented shift from "pedigree is sufficient" to "pedigree plus market thesis plus some form of prototype evidence" as the threshold for meeting with VCs. The ex-Riot premium held better than ex-ATVI through 2024, likely because Riot alumni are associated with a single coherent product culture (League of Legends, competitive live service) that investors find more legible.

## Context

InvestGame is a games industry data and investment analysis firm. This analysis is part of their "Alumni Effect" series covering multiple studio pedigree cohorts (Riot, ATVI, Take-Two). The series provides the most systematic publicly available dataset on what pedigree does to deal economics in game studio formation fundraising.

A companion Take-Two alumni analysis found mobile-focused alumni commanded $13.3M average checks versus $6.4M for PC/console-focused founders from the same studio -- genre-platform alignment with the investor thesis matters as much as the studio name.

## Applicability

**For NBI:** Three uses:

1. **Sizing the ask.** When an NBI client with ex-Riot or comparable credentials is setting their raise target, the 53% premium over $7.3M industry average gives a defensible anchor for why they should target $10-12M rather than $5-7M at seed.

2. **Investor targeting.** a16z appears in 13 ex-Riot rounds with $339M cumulative value -- they are the documented first-call institutional investor for this cohort. BITKRAFT appears consistently as co-investor. For ex-ATVI alumni, Griffin Gaming Partners and Galaxy Interactive appear alongside a16z.

3. **Calibrating expectations.** The 2023-2024 compression (ex-ATVI from $10.7M to $4.2M) is a warning that the premium compresses under market pressure. The 2025-2026 pitch must carry a market thesis alongside the pedigree claim to hold the premium.

**Applicable when:** Any NBI client with a major studio background (Riot, Blizzard, Activision, Bungie, id Software) asking how to size their raise or which VCs to target first. Also useful as a reality check for clients who believe pedigree alone will carry the raise -- the failure cases in the cohort show it accelerates access but does not guarantee success.
