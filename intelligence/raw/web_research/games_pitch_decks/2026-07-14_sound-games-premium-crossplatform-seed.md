# Sound Games -- Premium Cross-Platform Studio -- Seed -- Raised $6.5M from Point72 Ventures -- January 2026

## Key Content

Sound Games (Seattle, Washington; seed stage) raised $6.5M in January 2026, led by Point72 Ventures, with participation from Timeless, Daybreak, WOCstar, Hustle Fund, ZVC, and other investors.

**Founders:**
- Sergio Abril and Jacobo Abril: previously co-founded nada studio, a premium mobile and PC game developer. Prior nada studio titles include Starman: Tale of Light and Flocks -- premium-priced games on PC and mobile.
- Mike Schmid (CEO): former head of publishing at Rec Room (Seattle); former VP of Growth at Backbone (mobile gaming hardware); spent four years at Apple.

**Pitch thesis -- "Pay Once, Play Anywhere":**

The pitch was built around a single coherent market thesis rather than a single game: players buy a title once and access it across PC, console, and mobile without re-purchasing on each platform. The studio positioned this as a direct response to what they characterised as the erosion of the premium gaming market by free-to-play, subscription services, and heavy in-game monetisation models.

The argument: there is a meaningful market segment of quality-focused players who want to own games outright -- not rent them through subscriptions, not unlock content through IAP, not be extracted from via live-service mechanics. This segment is systematically underserved as major AAA publishers have migrated to F2P and GaaS models.

The pitch succeeded on three elements:
1. **Market thesis clarity.** A single coherent idea (premium ownership + cross-platform access) that VCs can underwrite without needing to evaluate a single game's merit.
2. **Team with operational evidence.** Founders had shipped premium games commercially (Sergio/Jacobo Abril via nada studio) and held publishing/growth leadership at scale gaming companies (Schmid via Rec Room and Backbone). The team could credibly execute on both the game quality and the platform distribution sides of the thesis.
3. **First title in market before the raise.** Go Ape Ship! (a cosy co-op multiplayer game for 1-8 players, playable across Steam, Epic Games Store, Google Play, and App Store) was announced with a February 18, 2026 launch date at the time of the seed announcement. A shipping product alongside the raise reduced execution risk substantially.

Point72 Ventures' investment rationale (public statement): confidence that the "pay once, play anywhere" model and focus on quality create room for a different kind of company in the current gaming market.

## Decisions / Insights

- Studio-level VC pitch (thesis about a model) versus game-level publisher pitch (thesis about a specific product) remains the primary division in gaming fundraising. Sound Games is a studio-model pitch: the VC is investing in a market thesis and a team, not a specific title.
- Anti-F2P positioning as a distinct VC thesis is now documented. "Premium quality over extraction" is investable when backed by a coherent argument about underserved market segments and a team with operational credibility. The model requires the team to actually ship quality, not just argue for it.
- Cross-platform simultaneous launch (PC + console + mobile, single purchase) is a technical differentiator that requires specific publishing infrastructure. Studios pitching this model must demonstrate they can actually execute the cross-platform delivery -- the nada studio founders' prior experience with PC/mobile premium titles provided this credential.
- First title launched before the seed close is now a documented pitch strategy for premium studios: reduce narrative risk by having something in market, then use the seed to fund the pipeline.

## Context

Sound Games is part of a Seattle gaming cluster that has produced Rec Room, Backbone, and several mobile gaming startups. Point72 Ventures is the venture arm of Steve Cohen's Point72 Asset Management -- a crossover fund with deep capital that has been selectively entering gaming investment. Go Ape Ship! launched February 18, 2026 simultaneously on Steam, Epic Games Store, Google Play Store, and Apple App Store -- a genuine cross-platform launch at seed stage.

The raise was announced in January 2026. The studio is actively building its game pipeline beyond the first title using the seed capital.

## Applicability

**For NBI:** Use when advising a studio pitching premium quality as a differentiator against the F2P market. The Sound Games pitch structure (thesis + team track record + first title in market) is replicable for any studio that has shipped premium games and can argue for an underserved quality-focused segment. Also relevant when advising studios on cross-platform launch strategies -- simultaneous PC/console/mobile at seed stage is unusual enough to function as a structural moat claim.

**Applicable when:** Client is a premium studio positioning against F2P or subscription market; client is pitching to VC rather than publisher and needs a studio-level thesis rather than a game-level pitch; any studio whose founding team includes prior premium game shipping experience and can argue a market gap in quality-focused content.
