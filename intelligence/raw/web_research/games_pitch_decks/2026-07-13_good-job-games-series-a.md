# Good Job Games -- Mobile Match-3 (Match Villains) -- Seed + Series A -- Raised $83M total from Menlo Ventures + Anthos Capital + Bessemer

## Key Content

Good Job Games was founded by Ilker Ilicali (CEO) and Nazim Akmandil (President). They bootstrapped the studio for eight years, building one of the largest hypercasual portfolios globally -- reaching over 2 billion players across their output. Their hypercasual catalogue included Zen Match (subsequently sold). The pivot to mid-core match-3 was a deliberate strategic repositioning: hypercasual was declining structurally due to IDFA/AAID deprecation compressing ad revenue, rising CPIs, and download growth decoupling from monetisation. Match-3 offered higher LTV, stronger retention, and proven billion-dollar franchise examples as category validation (Royal Match at $1B+/year, Candy Crush at multi-billion).

Match Villains launched globally November 2024. Menlo Ventures led a $23M seed round in March 2025, with Arcadia Gaming Partners co-investing. The seed pitch centred on the bootstrapped operational track record applied to a specific genre thesis. Menlo partner Amy Wu Martin's diligence process included personally playing 1,000+ levels and concluding Match Villains had "the highest player retention in the category." The primary quantitative hook at seed: highest D30 retention in match-3 on the App Store.

Seven months after the game's global launch -- four months after the seed closed -- Menlo co-led a $60M Series A (July 31, 2025) alongside Anthos Capital, with Bessemer Venture Partners participating. The interval between seed and Series A was approximately four months, unusually fast. The reason stated by Menlo: "Beat every metric they forecasted since the Seed round."

Verified Series A metrics: 350,000+ daily active users, $15.6M in IAP revenue since November 2024 launch ($12.7M of that generated after the March 2025 seed round), and highest D30 retention in match-3 on the App Store maintained through user acquisition scaling. Anthos partner Zack Zaharis stated: "It's rare to see this level of polish and traction so early."

The founding team's eight-year bootstrapped history created a specific operational advantage: they already ran mobile games at scale (live ops, UA, ASA, data infrastructure) before taking external capital. The VC funding was applied to genre-transition execution, not to building foundational capabilities. The pitch was therefore not "give us money to build this" -- it was "we already know how to operate this; give us money to scale."

## Decisions / Insights

- Eight years bootstrapped created the operational infrastructure that made the seed metrics possible. Investors were funding genre expansion, not capability building -- a structurally lower-risk bet than backing a team without a live operations track record.
- D30 retention is the explicitly named primary KPI for match-3 investment at Menlo. For NBI advisory: when a client pitches a casual or match-3 studio, D30 is the metric that moves the conversation.
- Hypercasual-to-mid-core pivot is a fundable thesis when founders bring operational maturity. The risk Menlo took was genre execution risk, not people risk -- and the bootstrapped track record de-risked people.
- Committing to specific forecasts at seed and beating them by Series A is the documented path to a fast follow-on. The four-month seed-to-Series-A was a direct consequence of forecast outperformance, not relationship management.

## Context

Good Job Games is Turkey-based. Match Villains reached $20M+ in lifetime revenue by mid-2025 and was climbing the match-3 App Store charts. The studio's trajectory is part of the broader Turkish mobile cluster (Peak Games alumni at TaleMonster, Dream Games, Grand Games) that has accumulated institutional credibility with US and European gaming VCs. Series A capital is in deployment for live service expansion and new titles.

## Applicability

**For NBI:** Use this case when advising a bootstrapped mobile studio considering first institutional capital. The Good Job Games narrative is among the most replicable in the bank: operational depth from bootstrapping translates directly into investor confidence at seed stage. It is also the clearest evidence of what Menlo Ventures specifically evaluates for mobile game investment (D30 as primary retention signal, forecast commitment and delivery as Series A trigger).

**Applicable when:** NBI client is a bootstrapped mobile studio (any genre) pivoting to mid-core or match-3; or any mobile studio with live metrics that beat prior projections. Also use when client asks about typical seed-to-Series-A timing -- four months is the documented fastest path, but it required beating all seed-stage forecasts.
