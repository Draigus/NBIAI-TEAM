# Wayfinder Games -- EA DICE / Embark Alumni, Makers Fund Lead, Behold Ventures + Dreamhaven Co-Investors, Seed 2023

## Key Content

Wayfinder Games was announced in February 2023 as a new independent studio based in Umeå, Sweden, operating fully remote.

**Founders:**
- Dennis Brännvall (Game Director) -- former creative director for Star Wars Battlefront at EA DICE
- Fia Tjernberg (Studio Director/CEO) -- EA DICE, Embark Studios
- Adam Clark (Production Director) -- EA DICE, Embark Studios
- Manne Ederyd (Technical Director) -- EA DICE, Embark Studios; also Anarchy Online

All four founders had worked together previously at DICE and/or Embark before forming Wayfinder. The studio had 18 developers at the time of announcement.

**Funding:** Undisclosed amount. Investors: Makers Fund (lead), Behold Ventures, Dreamhaven (strategic co-investor).

**Game concept:** Original RPGs "combining elements from the entire spectrum of the genre" -- positioned to appeal to both tabletop RPG enthusiasts and MMO communities. Mission: shared storytelling experiences that evoke collaborative play.

**Makers Fund investor quote (Archie Stonehill, Principal at Makers Fund, on record):**
"Dennis, Fia, Adam, and Manne founded Wayfinder not only as supremely talented developers, but as close friends and colleagues who loved working together -- that collaborative culture has helped them attract a diverse group of talented individuals from all over the world."

This is a direct, named investor statement about what specifically moved the needle: pre-formed team chemistry and documented collaboration history, not just cumulative credits.

## Decisions / Insights

The Makers Fund quote is the most important data point in this extract. It is the only on-record statement from a top-tier gaming VC specifying that **team chemistry and collaboration history** was the decisive investment factor at formation stage -- not individual credentials.

This matters for NBI because it confirms a hypothesis that is intuitive but rarely documented: VCs investing in formation studios are not just buying a list of credits. They are buying the theory that this specific group of people can execute together under pressure. A team that has shipped titles together, had creative disagreements and resolved them, and chosen to work together again is a different proposition from a team assembled for the pitch.

**Three investor-stack components and what each provides:**
1. Makers Fund (lead) -- global specialist gaming VC ($500M+ Fund III, March 2022). Provides institutional credibility, LP network, and global distribution relationships
2. Behold Ventures -- Nordic specialist VC run by Karl Magnus Troedsson (former DICE/Battlefield studio head). Provides domain validation (Troedsson knows DICE culture and can personally evaluate the founders' track record) and Nordic investor ecosystem access
3. Dreamhaven (Mike/Amy Morhaime) -- strategic operator. Provides publishing network access, industry relationships, and a signal that another credible operator endorses the team

The three-tier syndicate structure (global specialist VC + geography-specialist VC + strategic operator) is replicable for European studio formations: each investor serves a different validation and resource function.

**Behold Ventures context:** Behold's first fund closed at oversubscribed levels. In February 2025 the European Investment Fund committed €20M to Behold -- the EIF's first-ever direct gaming sector investment. Behold is led by Karl Magnus Troedsson (DICE studio head, Battlefield franchise). They are explicitly focused on Nordic founders at early stage. Their co-investment in both Dead Astronauts (€4M seed, February 2025) and Wayfinder Games confirms them as the dominant first-call VC for Nordic studio formations.

## Context

Announced February 2023. All four founders came from DICE and Embark -- two studios with strong collaborative culture (Embark is known for a flat structure and long-tenure teams). The fact that Brännvall/Tjernberg/Clark/Ederyd collectively left Embark to found a new studio is itself a signal of conviction: they chose each other over remaining at an established employer.

Dreamhaven's involvement as co-investor positions the studio within the Morhaime publishing network -- relevant for distribution conversations and potential follow-on capital.

## Applicability

**For NBI:** Two direct applications:

1. **Nordic clients:** Behold Ventures is the first-call institutional investor for any Nordic studio formation. Their EIF backing institutionalises their position. The Dead Astronauts and Wayfinder investments confirm they fund pre-prototype teams with verifiable credits from major Nordic studios (DICE, Embark, Massive Entertainment/Ubisoft).

2. **All formation clients:** The Makers Fund quote is the most usable articulation of what distinguishes a strong formation pitch from a list of credits. The pitch conversation should centre on: how long has this team worked together, what have they shipped together, why did they choose each other again, and what does the team's history tell you about how they navigate conflict and creative disagreement?

**Applicable when:** NBI client is a team -- not a solo founder -- with documented prior collaboration history. The Makers Fund rationale applies regardless of geography. For Nordic-based teams specifically, Behold Ventures is the primary institutional target; the global gaming VCs (Makers Fund, 1Up Ventures) are attainable as co-investors once the Nordic lead is secured.
